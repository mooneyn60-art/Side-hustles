## How to fill it in
Each evening, open Etsy Shop Manager > Marketing > Etsy Ads, set the date range to "today," and copy the numbers per listing exactly as shown. Don't estimate. Leave a cell blank if Etsy hasn't reported it yet.

- **Views** = ad impressions (Etsy "Views")
- **Clicks** = ad clicks
- **CTR** = clicks ÷ views
- **Spend** = ad spend ($)
- **Orders** = orders Etsy attributes to ads
- **Revenue** = ad revenue ($)
- **ROAS** = revenue ÷ spend

## Day 5 check (keep / stop per listing)
These are decision rules I'm suggesting, not benchmarks from shop data (the shop has no ad history yet).

- **Stop a listing** if it has spent its share (about $1.50+) with **0 clicks**. People see it but don't want it, so the photo or title needs work, not more spend.
- **Stop a listing** if it has **10+ clicks and 0 orders, and its favorites didn't go up**. The listing page isn't converting (check price, photos, and shipping-date copy).
- **Keep** any listing with at least 1 order, or clicks that are still climbing.
- **Total spend check:** if cumulative spend is already over $15 by day 5, cut the daily budget back to $3 if Etsy allows, and flag it to Moondog88.
- Money from a stopped listing doesn't go to the others. The budget stays at or under $3/day.

## Day 10 check (end of test)
- **Turn ads off on all listings** (the approval covers 10 days only).
- For each listing, write down total views, clicks, CTR, spend, orders, revenue, and ROAS.
- **Keep-worthy (propose continuing):** revenue at or above spend (ROAS ≥ 1.0), and profit per order after Etsy fees and Printify cost still clears the 45% margin rule at the ad cost per order. Printify costs are still [CONFIRM], so this can't be scored until they're filled in.
- **Fix before re-testing:** has clicks but no orders.
- **Drop from ads:** few or no clicks after the full test.
- Send Moondog88 a one-paragraph summary with the real numbers and a yes/no recommendation for any continued spend. Continuing needs a new okay.

## Hard stops (any day)
- Cumulative spend reaches $30
- Dec 5 (pause physical items; printables may keep running only if Moondog88 okays it separately)
- A listing goes inactive or sells out at Printify
- Moondog88 says stop
