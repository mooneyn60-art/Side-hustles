# FurEver Faces TikTok formats (@fureverfaces)

Shop marketing for the FurEver Faces Etsy shop and Shopify store (egayix-ff.myshopify.com).
Strictly separate from the Meatball Myths channels: no Meatball branding, characters, voice or footage.

**Voice:** warm, wry doodle-parent humor. The dog runs the house; we just pay for it.
**Hero line:** "For people owned by a doodle."
**Every video:** silent MP4 from the pipeline, 7–15 s, 9:16, text inside the safe zone, small footer
"FurEver Faces · art made with AI tools."

## Rules that apply to every format
- **Audio:** TikTok Commercial Music Library tracks or original audio only (this is business promo;
  trending licensed songs are not cleared for brand accounts). The MP4s ship silent. Metricool can't
  attach TikTok sounds, so audio is added in the TikTok app before posting. Keep `autoAddMusic` off
  so TikTok doesn't pick a sound.
- **CTA:** "Link in bio," pointing to both the Etsy shop and the Shopify store (bio link to be set up
  by Moondog88).
- **Disclosures:** turn on TikTok's content disclosure, "Your brand" (promoting our own business),
  on every post (`commercialContentOwnBrand: true`). Art is made with AI tools: say so in the
  on-screen footer and the caption, and set TikTok's AI-generated label (`isAigc: true`).
  Lifestyle composites (flat-lays, unboxing hands, tree scenes) are AI-made scenes, not customer photos.
- **Banned:** "free shipping"; fake reviews, star ratings, "best seller," or fake scarcity ("only 3
  left," "selling out," "last chance"); any price that doesn't match the live store that day (safest:
  no prices in video, "see link in bio"); any QR code, coupon or discount code aimed at Etsy buyers;
  any claim not in the product facts (no invented colors, fabrics, sizes or countries).
- **Facts we can use:** tees are unisex Bella + Canvas cotton, S to 2XL. Mugs are ceramic, 11 oz or
  15 oz, dishwasher and microwave safe. Ornaments are round ceramic with ribbon. Everything is printed
  to order. Order by Dec 5 for Christmas delivery; after that we still make and ship it, but it may
  arrive after Dec 25. Bandanas have no size or fabric facts on file, so don't claim any.
- **Hashtags:** 3–5 per post, always including `#fureverfaces`. No #fyp spam.

## F1. Product mockup slideshow
**Use for:** one hero product (ornament, mug, tee). 4 slides, about 10–11 s.

**Structure**
1. Lifestyle or 9:16 crop, full-bleed, with the hook card (3 s).
2. Clean mockup, with the product name on top and the slogan on the bottom (2.8 s).
3. Second angle, with one true product fact (2.6–2.8 s).
4. Lifestyle shot, with "Order by Dec 5 for Christmas delivery" (in season) and the CTA card (3 s).

- "Your doodle is going on the tree this year."
- "He's on the nice list. Pending."
- "The mug for people whose dog has opinions."

`Art made with AI tools. Printed to order. Link in bio for our shop and Etsy.`
