## Production and Christmas cutoff

*Same on Etsy and Shopify.*

Each item is printed to order. Processing takes 2–5 business days, then shipping takes 3–8 days.

To receive your order in time for Christmas, please order by December 5. Orders placed after that date are still made and shipped, but Christmas delivery can't be guaranteed.
