## Top 5 rules (2026 best practice in this field, each with a source URL)
1. Reply to 95% of first messages in a thread within 24 hours; Etsy reviews Star Seller eligibility on the 1st of each month using the prior three months, and also requires at least 5 orders and $300 in sales plus 90 days since the first sale. https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/4403058372503-What-is-the-Star-Seller-Badge
2. Ship 95% of orders on time — meaning within the processing time set on the listing — with valid tracking or an Etsy-purchased label when required (the minimum customer-service standard is 80% on-time, and tracking is not required for that minimum). https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/4403058372503-What-is-the-Star-Seller-Badge
3. Respond to every Help with Order message within 48 hours, ship on time with tracking, and work cases in Shop Manager: buyers may open a case 48 hours after contacting us (7+ days after the maximum estimated delivery date for non-delivery), then have 30 days to open it; Etsy Purchase Protection can cover up to $250 of a qualifying refund. https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/5850122619287-What-is-Etsy-s-Purchase-Protection-for-Sellers
4. You may ask for a review after delivery, but do not offer incentives and do not ask for a positive or 5-star rating; buyers have 100 days from the delivery date or estimated delivery date (or from download for digital items). Review window: https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360000572708-How-the-Review-System-Works-for-Sellers — I could not re-open Etsy's Seller Handbook or Anti-Shilling legal pages on Nov 16, 2026 (etsy.com returned a DataDome 403), so the "no incentives / no specific rating" line is our standing shop rule; Help Center does say asking for a post-purchase review is allowed and that shilling or falsely inflating a review score is not (https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115015654988-How-to-Send-Messages-to-Buyers and https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115015808588-What-to-Do-if-You-Receive-a-Negative-Review).
5. Keep December 5 as the shop's Christmas order-by date, but do not guarantee delivery: Printify's official production window is 2–7 business days (our promise is 2–5) and US Standard shipping is typically 2–5 business days (our promise is 3–8 business days); Printify's 2026 recommended Standard submission cutoff for arrival by Dec 24 is Dec 9 at Monster Digital, Dec 8 at Printed Mint, and Dec 7 at SPOKE, while USPS Ground Advantage's recommended send-by for delivery before Dec 25 is Dec 17. Printify production and Standard shipping: https://help.printify.com/hc/en-us/articles/4483629751825-What-are-Printify-s-production-times-like and https://help.printify.com/hc/en-us/articles/4483626155409-What-are-Printify-s-shipping-options.
