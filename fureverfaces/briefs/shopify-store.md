## Top 5 rules (2026 best practice in this field, each with a source URL)
1. Keep the storefront in Google's "good" Core Web Vitals band at the 75th percentile — LCP at or under 2.5 seconds, INP at or under 200 ms, CLS at or under 0.1 — and do not lazy-load the LCP image; mark it `fetchpriority="high"` and render essential product content in Liquid/HTML, not client-side JavaScript. Sources: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/core-web-vitals and https://shopify.dev/docs/storefronts/themes/best-practices/performance
2. Give every page a unique, descriptive title (aim for 60 characters or fewer, 70 max in admin) and a unique meta description (about 160 characters), and write descriptive image alt text in readable phrases, not keyword lists. Source: https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/promoting-marketing/seo/adding-keywords
3. Do not treat Shopify product pages that only link out to Etsy as Google merchant-listing pages, and do not point Shopify canonicals at Etsy listings to "solve" duplicate content; Google says only pages where a shopper can purchase are eligible for merchant listings, and a cross-site `rel=canonical` is a hint, not a guarantee. Make Shopify copy and brand content clearly different from the matching Etsy listings. Sources: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/merchant-listing and https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/canonicalization-troubleshooting
4. Build trust with accessible contact details, reviews or testimonials, and clear, accurate policies; keep shipping and refund language aligned with "buy on Etsy," and do not advertise free shipping except with the approved US $49 wording. Source: https://www.shopify.com/blog/what-makes-a-good-website
5. Keep Shopify checkout off until Moondog88 approves turning it back on. The site already pays for Basic ($29/month billed yearly, or $39 billed monthly). A US Etsy sale costs a $0.20 listing fee, a 6.5% transaction fee on item + shipping + gift wrap, and 3% + $0.25 payment processing; a Shopify Payments card sale on Basic is 2.9% + 30¢, so Shopify is cheaper per order only when this site can acquire and convert its own buyers and we can handle split fulfillment. There is no official traffic threshold in the sources below; current volume (6 visitors in three live days) is too low to justify it. Measure "Buy on Etsy" as outbound click events (GA4 Enhanced Measurement `click` with `outbound=true`, filtered to etsy.com). Sources: https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115014483627-What-are-the-Fees-and-Taxes-for-Selling-on-Etsy , https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115015628847-What-are-Payment-Processing-Fees-for-Selling-on-Etsy , https://www.shopify.com/pricing , and https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/9216061?hl=en
