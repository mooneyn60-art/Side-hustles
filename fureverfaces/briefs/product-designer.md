## Top 5 rules (2026 best practice in this field, each with a source URL)
1. Sell breed-specific identity and humor (Doodle Mom, Doodle Dad, era jokes, matching pet/owner sets), not generic “I love dogs.” Source: https://www.sellermockups.com/blog/best-print-on-demand-niches-etsy
2. Build print files at 300 DPI for the real print size (pixels = inches × 300), as RGB/sRGB transparent PNG, and always confirm the exact area in Printify Product Creator — typical working canvases are 4500×5400 px for a 15×18 in chest and about 2475×1155 px for a common 11 oz mug wrap; large AOP items such as bandanas may allow 120–150 DPI, so do not invent a size, open the template. Sources: https://help.printify.com/hc/en-us/articles/4483617936657-What-type-of-print-files-does-Printify-require · https://printify.com/knowledge-hub/dpi-for-printing/ · https://printify.com/guide/design-guide/
3. Keep every pixel fully opaque or fully transparent: no soft glows, feathered shadows, or fades to transparency — DTG lays a white underbase under any partial pixel, which shows up as chalky halos, grain, or faded color, especially on dark or colored shirts. Sources: https://help.printify.com/hc/en-us/articles/51387270695953-Why-does-my-print-look-different-from-my-design · https://printify.com/knowledge-hub/master-dtg-underbase-for-dark-apparel/
4. Treat platform photo rules as different jobs: Etsy listing photos should be at least 2000 px, square or landscape, accurate, and (for most items) include one unedited photo of the actual product, though authorized stock mockups are allowed for POD; Shopify should lead with a clear, high-quality hero; TikTok Shop requires a front-view main image on a pure white background, ≥600×600, with no added text or logos, and does not allow placeholders or digital renderings — lifestyle and styled scenes belong in additional slots, and real sample photos are the safe TikTok path. Sources: https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115015663347-Requirements-and-Best-Practices-for-Images-in-Your-Etsy-Shop · https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/44297885802647-How-Do-Etsy-s-Listing-Image-Requirements-Apply-to-My-Listings · https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/products/product-media/product-photography · https://seller-us.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=3196690250417921&lang=en
5. Design for the thumbnail first: the headline and tagline must still read at ~200 px, and mug/ornament type must avoid hairline detail (Printify: keep artwork strokes at least about 5 pixels). Sources: https://printify.com/guide/design-guide/ · https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115015663347-Requirements-and-Best-Practices-for-Images-in-Your-Etsy-Shop
