## Top 5 rules (2026 best practice in this field, each with a source URL)
1. Open with a product-related hook in the first 3–6 seconds, using bold on-screen text or voiceover so viewers know what the video is about before they scroll; our tighter house cut (motion or a face in frame 1, product on screen by 1.5 s) stays in force because this account's cartoon lost most viewers at 0:01. Source: [TikTok Shop Academy, Content Styles (updated May 20, 2026)](https://seller-us.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=4401155330393899&lang=en).
2. Optimize every post for watch time and completion: TikTok says a strong interest signal is whether a viewer finishes a video, and that neither follower count nor prior hit videos are direct For You ranking factors, so a 3-follower account is not blocked by size. Source: [TikTok Newsroom, How TikTok recommends videos #ForYou](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-us/how-tiktok-recommends-videos-for-you).
3. Formats that convert show the product early, pair features with benefits, and demonstrate it in use (multi-angle, results, people and motion)—not a still-image slideshow for the whole video. Source: [TikTok Shop Academy, "Creating Good Quality TikTok Shop Videos" (updated May 14, 2026)](https://seller-us.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=1314959099512618&lang=en).
4. Turn on the content disclosure setting as "your brand" on every shop ad so the post is labeled Promotional content; TikTok says this label does not affect feed distribution. For AI visuals, use only TikTok's built-in AI-generated toggle (`isAigc`); do not mention AI in captions, on screen, or in hashtags. Sources: [Promoting a brand, product, or service](https://support.tiktok.com/en/business-and-creator/creator-and-business-accounts/promoting-a-brand-product-or-service); [AI-generated content](https://support.tiktok.com/en/using-tiktok/creating-videos/ai-generated-content).
5. Posting more often does not change For You ranking; capture attention early, hold it, and give a new video a couple of days before judging it. TikTok has not published a universal best time, so keep 10 AM / 3 PM / 6 PM ET until Studio audience hours unlock at 100 followers. Source: [TikTok Newsroom, 5 tips for TikTok creators](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-us/5-tips-for-tiktok-creators).
