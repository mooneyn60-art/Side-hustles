## Top 5 rules (2026 best practice in this field, each with a source URL)
1. **Fix organic Etsy search before paying for clicks.** Etsy says ads "are unlikely to turn a low-demand listing into a top seller." New shops have a 15-day wait, the minimum is $1/day, and Etsy suggests judging a strategy over 30 days. Target 2026 demand terms: pet gifting searches have doubled and pet ornaments are up 22%.
   - https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360033701174-How-to-Set-Up-and-Manage-an-Etsy-Ads-Campaign
   - https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/1521255894814
2. **Send every social link through Share & Save.** Etsy refunds the full 6.5% transaction fee on these orders from Oct 20, 2026 to Jan 4, 2027 (4% after that). Links in Etsy Messages or listing descriptions don't count, and an Offsite Ads last click overrides the refund.
   - https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/16981332744087-How-to-Save-on-Etsy-Fees-with-the-Share-Save-Program
3. **Price Offsite Ads into the margin math.** The fee is 15% for shops under $10K (opt-out allowed) and 12% at $10K+ (mandatory), capped at $100 per order, with a 30-day window. The 45% ceilings don't include it.
   - https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360000338367-How-Etsy-s-Offsite-Ads-Work
4. **Build TikTok ads the way TikTok specifies:**
   - Show the product in the first 3 seconds and land the hook within 6.
   - 9:16 with sound and 5–10 words per second on screen.
   - Real people and product footage; put CTAs in the caption.
   - 21–34 seconds for in-feed.
   - Post 2–5 times a week. Buffer found +17% views per post compared with once a week.
   - Promote only videos that are already working. TikTok publishes no minimum; about $3/day is typical.
   - https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/creative-best-practices
   - https://buffer.com/resources/how-often-should-you-post-on-tiktok/
   - https://tokconnect.com/guides/does-tiktok-promote-work/
5. **Plan promos around the real calendar:**
   - Thanksgiving weekend (64% of shoppers do most of their buying then).
   - Printify US Standard order cutoffs of Dec 7–9 (ours is Dec 5).
   - Don't pitch past buyers in Etsy Messages.
   - Keep the original price in place before a sale.
   - https://nrf.com/media-center/press-releases/affordability-shapes-winter-holiday-shopping-according-to-nrf-holiday-survey
   - https://bestprintondemand.co/suppliers/printify/holiday-deadlines
   - https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360039531534-How-to-Participate-in-Sales-Events-on-Etsy
