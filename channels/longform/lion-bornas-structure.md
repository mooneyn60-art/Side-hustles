## Mid-roll check: ~2:07, 3:41, 5:27

These are the three **primary** slots already in the v2 script (chapter-gap breaks of 0.3 s, never mid-sentence). Times below are the JSON `_midroll_after.at` values at the 194.5 wpm clock.

YouTube policy used here, and only this, from support.google.com:

- Mid-rolls can be turned on for **monetized videos that are 8 minutes or longer**. ([Manage mid-roll ad breaks in long videos](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6175006))
- Manual slots should sit at **natural breakpoints** (a pause in audio or a visual transition). Slots that are **mid-sentence or mid-action** are less likely to serve ads. (same page)
- YouTube, not the placement itself, decides whether an ad serves. (same page)

This scripted runtime is **8:22.1 at 194.5 wpm — only about 22 seconds over the 8:00 line.** The speech-only clock is 8:10.1. A faster-than-194.5 TTS pass, or cutting the 12 s end hold without a slower render to lean on, could put the file under 8:00 and make manual mid-rolls unavailable. The slower ~180 wpm polar-pace estimate (~9:01.3) has more room. **Confirm duration on the actual render before placing slots. Do not trim v2’s added runtime lines (gestation, males’ hunt share, nomad herds, pride size) or the end hold just to tighten pace until that confirm is in.**

`pause_lines` in the JSON (`s3.l2`, `s9.l2`, `s11.l3`) are dramatic holds — cub-killing, Tanzania attacks, and the verdict. **They are not ad breaks.** Do not place mid-rolls on them.
