## (a) Franchise bible: "Noodle & Sauce's House Guests #N"

**Why:** per Trends, the 3 Bugs long-form had a 5.44% CTR and 42.7% viewed, and its sequels 4.6% and 4.4% (Trends' word; see flag 4). Research's §2 tactic is "run it into the dirt": a numbered, named franchise in most slots until a 28-day median drops.

- **Name and number:**
  - The franchise is "Noodle & Sauce's House Guests". Each episode is numbered: #1, #2, …
  - The number lives in the description's first line, the playlist ("House Guests"), and a "#N" corner tag on the thumbnail.
  - The title itself follows the house pattern (week 1 rule: no series tags in titles). See flag 1.
- **Format (8–10 min, comedy or curious, never peril):**
  1. Cold open: "you" plus a clock, with one house bug or spider found in one room.
  2. The way inside the narrating pet's body, which gives the episode's POV.
  3. Three questions, in order: *Why is it here? What is it? What do you do?*
  4. A twist that corrects one common mix-up.
  5. **The verdict card**, which is the episode's safety line, plus the if-bitten or if-unsure line, both said clean.
  6. A callback, then a tease of House Guests #(N+1).
- **Who narrates which bug.** This is cartoon casting, not a claim about the pets' senses.
  - **Noodle ("Inside Noodle's Nose")** takes floor-level house guests: spiders on the floor or in the tub, roaches, centipedes. Nose facts are limited to DB32–DB33 (AKC).
  - **Sauce ("Inside Sauce's Eyes / Whiskers")** takes climbers, jumpers and corner-web spiders: cellar spiders, jumping spiders, window bugs. A Sauce body fact needs its own source first (UNV-F1).
- **The verdict system** replaces "kill / don't kill" with stamp cards. Each card carries its source's condition:
  - **LEAVE IT:** only where a source says so, for that species and place (e.g. WSU, giant house spider, HS37).
  - **JAR IT OUT:** UC IPM's jar-and-paper release, only for a spider you've already ruled out as a widow or recluse, never with bare hands (HS71, W5).
  - **GET IT IDENTIFIED:** don't guess a widow or recluse, and use a Cooperative Extension office (HS62, HS59–60).
  - **IF BITTEN:** wash the bite and use cold compresses. Unusual or severe reaction → physician, hospital or poison control, 1-800-222-1222 (HS64).
  - "Call a pro if…" is used **only** once Research sources a professional-help trigger (UNV-B3). Until then the card says "get it identified".
- **Rules (on top of the series rules):**
  1. **No gore and no peril.**
     - No bug is squished, sprayed, drowned, eaten or chased on screen.
     - The pets never touch, bat or eat the guest. They watch from a distance.
     - Meatball's shoe is a prop that never lands.
     - The guest exits alive in every episode: jarred out, walking off, or left alone. (UC IPM lists crushing and vacuuming as options in HS65. We don't stage them, and we don't call them wrong.)
  2. **The verdicts are safety lines.** Each keeps its source's condition, species and place, word for word. No blanket "harmless". A species verdict is never stretched to "the spider in your house".
  3. **No "kill it" titles or hooks** ("Should I Kill…" appears only as a searched question; see flag 1). There are no body counts, and no bug is "evil" or a "villain" (that would be a motive claim).
  4. **Vary the outcome** (YouTube's July 2026 templated/peril wording, week 1 research §3). Across episodes, rotate the guest's exit, the narrating pet, the room and the twist type (mix-up, myth, scale).
  5. **The fact source is a pack or Research row only.** Species facts, venom lines and verdicts are never invented. A bug with no pack row gets no episode until Research builds one.
  6. **One bug per episode**, so it never turns into the multi-spider ID long-form (`longform-spider-id`, drafted). Other species appear only as the "get it identified" lookalike beat.
