## Series rules (all three episodes)

- **Title pattern (one, all three; see "Retitle"):** `[Exact searched question]? Inside Noodle's/Sauce's [Body Part]`, 60 characters or fewer. No series tags, no "Ep N", no banned words.
- **Who's who:**
  - "You" = the viewer, as the owner.
  - Cartoon-ball Meatball (the live look, pfp-final-c) plays the owner on screen and stays the lead.
  - The body scenes are inside **his dog, Noodle** or **his cat, Sauce**. Write the comma form the first time in each video.
- **Organ characters** are labeled cartoon parts. They speak only in speech-bubble cards; Andrew narrates, with no new voices.
  - Every organ line is a sourced fact or a gag on Meatball. None states a pet's feeling or motive.
  - The narrator and the organs never present as a vet. Advice is always credited to its source (QA Y7).
- **Pet gags (bible):**
  - Noodle ends up with Meatball's food or prop. Sauce does the opposite of what Meatball wants.
  - Each is planted early and paid off late, as an action only.
  - Sauce's action changes from episode to episode (QA W-Y2).
- **Originality guardrail:**
  - One peril episode in three (EP 1). EP 2 is comedy; EP 3 is cozy.
  - No ending says a tactic saved anyone.
  - Movement in every scene, with a scene change every 20–30 s.
- **No reuse (QA Y4/T3):**
  - No shot, audio or render from these goes into any episode or cut: `chocolate-v6-yt`, `dog-chocolate-v6-yt` (#72), `dogs-lick-v6-yt`, `tts-dogs-lick`, `tt-dogs-lick`, `cats-knead-v6-yt`, `tts-cats-knead`, `tt-cats-knead`, the posted tail-wag Short #67, and `dog-teasers.md` §3 `dog-nose` (retired 11/16, replaced by EP 2 S1; QA E2-1).
  - No gag is lifted from them either.
  - Those drafts are **not** retired. The chapters below were cut back so that both can exist.
- **Long-form structure (MASTER_ORDERS "Watch hours" + research §7):**
  - narration from 0.0 s
  - a cold open with "you" and a clock
  - no joke, intro or comment ask before 0:25
  - part of the promise paid before 1:00
  - a new peak every 20–30 s
  - a midpoint re-hook
  - one corrected misconception
  - a sourced payoff and a clean safety line
  - a callback in the last ~20 s, then "watch this next"
  - no Subscribe/Follow card, no spoken 3-2-1
  - Pace: about 194.5 wpm.
- **Chapter cuts (ruling 1):**
  - About **3 YouTube Shorts and 3 TikToks per episode**, new beats only. That fits the 3-a-day Shorts cap and MASTER_ORDERS' "two or three … Shorts on the same creature (different facts, at least a day apart)".
  - **YouTube Short:** 16–18 s (52–58 words), at least 2 sourced facts plus a payoff, a v6 loop, no outro.
  - **TikTok:** 65–75 s (about 210–245 words). The first line is the searched phrase. Every second is new script: no slowed footage, looped footage or held frames (QA T2). The sweary pass comes from the Scriptwriter; attributions and safety lines are said clean.
- **How a cut points back to the episode:**
  - YouTube Short: Related video = the episode. The pinned comment is the corner-pill question plus "Full episode in the Related video 👆".
  - TikTok: a "Chapter N" title card on the first frame, and the caption line "Full episode: YouTube @MeatballMyths".
- **Opening shots (QA Y5 watch):**
  - The fur push-in plus porthole iris is used **once per long-form**, as the way inside.
  - Chapter cuts never open on it. Each cut below names its own first shot.
- **Fact IDs** refer to the packs (CH = chocolate, DL = dogs-lick, CK = cats-knead, DB = dog-bornas) and to Research's sourcing file (UNV-x, now sourced). Hedges and conditions stay word for word.
