## Series rules (all three episodes)

- **Title pattern (one, all three; changed 11/16 per Social Studio and `channels/search/title-pattern-1116.md`):** `[Exact searched question]? Inside Noodle's/Sauce's [Body Part]`, 60 characters or fewer. The question is the phrase people actually type into autocomplete. No series tags, no "Ep N". No banned words.
- **Who's who:** "you" = the viewer, as the owner. Meatball (stick figure) acts out the owner on screen and stays the lead. Inside-the-body scenes are inside **his dog, Noodle** or **his cat, Sauce**. Write the comma form the first time in each video.
- **Organ characters** are labeled cartoon parts (Stomach, Nose, Paws). They "speak" in speech-bubble cards. Andrew narrates; no new voices (voice lock). Every organ line is either a sourced fact or a gag on Meatball. It never states a pet's feeling or motive.
- **Pet gags (bible):** Noodle ends up with Meatball's food or prop. Sauce does the opposite of what Meatball wants and walks off. Plant one early, pay it off late. Actions only.
- **Originality guardrail (research #4):** one peril episode out of three (Ep 1). Ep 2 is comedy, Ep 3 is cozy. No ending says a tactic saved anyone. Movement in every scene, at least one scene change every 20–30 s.
- **Long-form structure (MASTER_ORDERS "Watch hours" + research §7):**
  - narration starts at 0.0 s
  - a cold open with "you" and a clock
  - no joke, intro or comment ask before 0:25
  - part of the promise paid before 1:00
  - a new peak every 20–30 s
  - a midpoint re-hook
  - one corrected misconception (the twist)
  - sourced payoff and a clean safety line
  - last ~20 s: callback, then "watch this next"
  - no Subscribe/Follow card, no spoken 3-2-1
  - Pace: about 194.5 wpm (house rate), so 8–10 min is about 1,550–1,950 words.
- **Chapter cuts:** each long-form gives 4–5 chapters. Each chapter has:
  - a **YouTube Short, 16–18 s** (52–58 words; lint range 16–20 s), with 2+ sourced facts and a payoff. It is a v6 loop with no outro.
  - a **TikTok, 65–75 s** (about 210–245 words; over 1 min for Rewards). The first line is the searched phrase. It gets the house sweary pass from the Scriptwriter, but attributions and safety lines are said clean.
- **How a cut points back to the episode:**
  - YouTube Short: Related video = the episode. The pinned comment is the corner-pill question plus "Full episode in the Related video 👆".
  - TikTok: a "Chapter N" title card in the first frame, and the caption line "Full episode: YouTube @MeatballMyths". No spoken outro.
- **Fact IDs** refer to the packs (CH = chocolate, DL = dogs-lick, CK = cats-knead, DB = dog-bornas). `UNV-x` = not verified; Research must source it or it gets cut. Hedges and conditions stay word for word.
