So the three timestamps below are where the **average viewer has left** (AVD), clustered across the long-forms with 40+ views. They are proxies, not measured cliffs.

1. **0:30.** How Wolves Became Dogs (0:30 of 8:21, with the most clicks of any long-form at 163) and Everything Is WRONG (0:34). People click on these and are gone within the intro.
2. **0:57–1:15.** Animals Thought Extinct (0:57), Weird Animal Facts (1:03), 3 More Bugs (1:06) and 3 Bugs (1:14). This is the biggest cluster. It roughly matches the end of the first list item or segment.

## 5. Three concrete fixes

1. **Make the first 0:30 deliver, not set up.** By 0:05, state the creature and the stakes ("this spider in your corner eats bigger spiders, so don't kill it"). Show the first real payoff by 0:20. Cut any channel intro, logo or "today we're going to…" before 0:30. This targets the Wolves-style exit at 0:30.
2. **Add an open loop at 0:55 and again at about 1:40.** Before item 1 ends, tease the scariest or weirdest item ("number 4 is the one in your bed right now") and say it comes later. Repeat the tease around 1:40. Order items as strong, then a cliffhanger, with the best one last. This targets the 0:57–1:15 and 1:45–2:10 exits.
3. **Make only house-bug and spider "kill it or keep it?" episodes, at 8–10 minutes, and pin a Short to each one.** It's the only format with both reach (the top 2 by hours) and the best hold. Stop CIA, conspiracy and generic myth compilations. Each daily Short should point to the matching episode with a related-video link, plus an end screen and playlist on the long-form, because view volume is the 190× gap. Longer episodes in the winning format raise the hours each view earns.
