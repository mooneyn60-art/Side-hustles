## Threats

1. **The ads-tier bar rises to 8,000 hours for new applicants on Feb 1, 2027, and we are at 12 hours with 75 days to Jan 31.** Rule source: `growth-plan-longform-shorts-1115.md` §3 (YouTube Help, checked 11/15), restated in baseline §1 and analytics §6. Verified live 11/17: YouTube Help 12843009, "Starting Feb. 1, 2027, YPP entry thresholds for new creators are changing to 8,000 qualified watch hours in the last 365 days, or 20M qualified Shorts views in the last 90 days, in addition to still needing 1k subscribers." Official hours: **12** (Earn card 11/17, data as of Nov 12). Arithmetic to 4,000 by Jan 31 is about 53 h/day (OUR ANALYSIS). Current measured long-form pace is 2.6–6.1 h/week, not 53 h/day. Applying after Jan 31 means facing 8,000 (or 20M Shorts views), not 4,000 (or 10M). Official pages say the new thresholds apply 'Starting Feb. 1, 2027' to 'new creators'. They don't say how an application submitted before Feb 1 but reviewed after is treated (not documented). This is the binding deadline in TASKS.md ("the real YPP deadline is 1/31/2027").

2. **YouTube Shorts have a hard ceiling on this channel, so Shorts cannot carry YPP.** 0 of 86 at 5x; best 2,445; 30K qualified Shorts views / 90d vs 10M (OFFICIAL). A breakout on TikTok did not produce a matching Shorts breakout (1,326 vs 71.1K). Relying on Shorts volume, or on a 0.49% Shorts-to-long-form pass-through, will not close 3,988 hours.

3. **A zero regular-viewer base means every long-form has to win cold, and failed packaging wastes the impressions we do get.** 99.3% new / 0.0% regular (OFFICIAL). *Weird Animal Facts* spent **5,262** impressions at 1.82% CTR and 11% viewed. If future house-bug long-forms are packaged like the quiz/myth videos, hours stay flat. **GUESS** as to algorithm punishment; the wasted impressions are evidence.
