## 4. `pantry-vs-clothes-moth` — title and hook (Thu 11/19 16:00 ET)

**Question asked:** Does UC IPM's Pantry Pests page support the title "Only One Eats Your Sweaters" (i.e., pantry moth larvae don't eat fabric)?

**Short answer:** **Yes, for pantry moth vs clothes moth**, by diet and by the page's "don't confuse" line — not by a verbatim "pantry moths don't eat fabric" sentence. Keep the first description line, which already says the adult clothes moth never eats and the larvae eat wool, fur, and silk.

**Sources opened:**

1. [UC IPM Pantry Pests](https://ipm.ucanr.edu/home-and-landscape/pantry-pests/) — Updated 03/2013.
2. [UC IPM Clothes Moths, Pest Notes 7435](https://ipm.ucanr.edu/home-and-landscape/clothes-moths/) — Updated 03/2013.
3. [UKY ENTFACT-609, Clothes Moths](https://entomology.mgcafe.uky.edu/ef609) — Revised 3/26.
