## 0. The one-paragraph answer

We aren't short on volume. We post 3 Shorts a day and about 4 TikToks a day. The problem is that **every format we run tops out around 1,000–2,000 views, and none of them earns money.** The small channels that broke out in the last 30 days all did the same three things: (1) **one character or "you" goes through a story with a clock running** (not a fact list); (2) **an 8–12 min long-form episode is the main product**, and Shorts or TikTok parts are trailers for it; (3) **one repeatable title pattern** they own. Two of them went from a few hundred subscribers to thousands in two weeks on one episode. **The real deadline is Jan 31, 2027, not end of month.** From Feb 1, new YouTube Partner Program applicants need **8,000** watch hours instead of 4,000 ([YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12843009?hl=en-12); [YouTube Blog](https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/youtube-partner-program-updates-2027-new-opportunities-earn/)). At our current pace, neither number is reachable. Only a long-form hit gets there.

   - "If Your Dog Ever… DON'T…"
   - "What Happens If You [do X to a bug]?"

## 3. Platform rules that matter for us (2025–2026, sourced)

### YouTube
- **YPP thresholds change Feb 1, 2027.** New applicants then need 1,000 subs plus **8,000 watch hours/365 days** or **20M Shorts views/90 days**. Until then it's 4,000 h or 10M ([YouTube Help 12843009](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12843009?hl=en-12); [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/streaming/977474/youtube-partner-program-new-requirements)).
  - The early-access tier (Fan Funding/Shopping) stays at **500 subs + 3,000 h or 3M Shorts views** (same Help page).
  - ANALYSIS: our Shorts route (10M views in 90 days) is ~140× off at ~79K views a month, so **long-form hours are the only realistic route.**
- **How Shorts are ranked:** first whether viewers choose to watch or swipe ("Stayed to watch", formerly "Viewed vs. swiped away"), then average view duration and percentage viewed, then likes and survey satisfaction ([Outformat, citing YouTube Help](https://outformat.com/blog/youtube-shorts-algorithm/); [Creativital](https://creativital.space/blog/youtube-shorts-algorithm)).
  - Since Aug 24, 2026, views count from the first frame. "Engaged views" is the old count and the one used for money; YouTube says recommendations didn't change ([Clyper](https://www.clyper.io/guides/youtube-shorts-algorithm)).
- **Shorts length data:**
  - Returbe, 48,804 Shorts from channels with 1K–200K subs: median views 25.0K–29.7K for ≤30 s vs **4.5K for 61–90 s**. Over 90 s recovers only for "a story with a payoff" ([Returbe, Oct 31, 2026](https://returbe.com/blog/how-long-should-a-youtube-short-be)).
  - vidIQ, 331M Shorts: 45–59 s had the highest *average* views and were 70% likelier to hit 1M than 15–29 s ([vidIQ](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/vidiq_we-analyzed-331-million-youtube-shorts-published-activity-7478837949355388930-moa_)).
  - ANALYSIS: keep YouTube Shorts ≤30 s for single-fact gags, and use 45–90 s only for real mini-stories (Sketching the Wild's 77–82 s hits).
- **Originality risk (high for us).** YouTube's July 2026 clarification names as not monetizable:
  - "videos where characters are put in the same situation over and over again with the same outcome"
  - "image slideshows and templated storylines"
  - "a series showing repetitive scenarios of animals in exaggerated distress or peril"
  - Source: [Tubefilter](https://www.tubefilter.com/2026/07/13/youtube-inauthentic-content-monetization-policy-update/); policy [1311392](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392).
  - "Would Meatball Survive?" as a fixed template, and our still-image Shorts, sit close to all three lines. The review happens when we apply, and it covers the whole channel.

### TikTok Creator Rewards
- Eligible videos are **original, over 1 minute, and have 1,000+ For You views.** A qualified view is 5+ s, unique per account, from one of 8 countries, and not "Not interested". Search views need 30+ s ([FastClip](https://fastclips.app/learn/tiktok-qualified-views); [blipix](https://blipix.pro/blog/how-much-does-tiktok-pay-creator-rewards)).
- **RPM = Standard Reward + Additional Reward.** The Additional Reward rises with **average watch time, retention at 30/40/50/60/90 s, completion rate and search value** (TikTok RPM explainer, Aug 18, 2026, summarized by [blipix](https://blipix.pro/blog/how-much-does-tiktok-pay-creator-rewards)).
  - The formula covers "originality, play duration, search value and audience engagement", and is not "favoring accounts with an excessive amount of videos" ([TikTok Newsroom](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-us/introducing-the-new-creator-rewards-program/)).
- **Search value:** TikTok's **Creator Search Insights** has a "Content gap" filter for "topics [that] are searched for often, but aren't featured in a large number of videos" ([TikTok Newsroom](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-US/creator-search-insights); [Creator Academy](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/en/article/Creator-Search-Insights)).
  - Our two best TikToks ever were search-led (FAA 17.6K, flights grounded 9.4K).
- **Benchmarks** for 60 s+ videos: completion of 20–35% is average, 35–50% good, 50%+ exceptional ([Viralo, third-party](https://viralo.studio/blog/video-retention-rate-benchmarks-2026)). Use our RPM dashboard to see where we sit.

## 5. Changes, ranked by expected impact

### #1: Launch "Meatball's Insides". Pet-POV story long-forms become the main product, at 3 episodes a week
- **The format:** 8–10 min. The cartoon Meatball (or Sauce) hits an everyday pet problem, and we cut inside his body or the house, where cartoon organs or bugs talk. A clock is on screen ("Hour 3 of 6").
  - The real science comes from vet and agency sources (FDA, AVMA, Merck Vet Manual are already in our fact packs).
  - Noodle and Sauce do action gags only.
- **Why:** The Zoo Inside got 72,337 views in 2 days at 315 subs with "Your Dog's Organs When She Holds Her Pee All Day" ([video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=boKPV_hm89g)). We already have the dog, the cast and the fact packs.
- **Hours math (ANALYSIS):** one 72K-view episode at a 3 min AVD ≈ 3,600 h, roughly 90% of our 4,000.
- **Keep** Things Living In Your House as the second long-form lane, but rebuild it as "What Happens If You ___?" experiments (the AreYouInterested model: 201K at 554 subs) instead of lists.

### #2: Every opening = "you" or Meatball + a clock + a consequence, inside 2 seconds (YouTube and TikTok)
- Rewrite all hooks to the pattern *"[Who] has [time] before [consequence]."*
- No fact-first openings.
- The payoff fact comes at the end, sourced, and safety lines keep their condition.
- **Why:** Sketching the Wild's *"You were born 6 minutes ago. You have 2 minutes to stand up…"* Short got 3,379,993 views ([Short](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/Lp6Im6SJamw)). Our Shorts lose viewers at a 7 s median AVD (Metricool).

### #3: Shorts and TikToks become trailers and chapters of the week's episode, not standalone facts
- **YouTube (keep 3 a day):**
  - 2 a day: ≤30 s gag or "myth correction" Shorts cut from the episode.
  - 1 a day: a 60–85 s second-person mini-story.
  - Each one's Related video points to the episode, as MASTER_ORDERS already says.
- **TikTok:**
  - 2–3 a day, never batches.
  - Each post is a 70–90 s **chapter** ("Part 2: Hour 4…") or a **search answer** built from Creator Search Insights "Content gap" topics. Say the searched phrase in the first line and the caption.
- **Why:**
  - Dreadify's 41 s Short of its own story got 363,539 views, more than the 10 min original ([Short](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oy5tgWti0YQ)).
  - Headlamp Guy's 88 s TikTok chapters have a 1,525 median vs our 233.
  - Search value and retention feed RPM ([TikTok Newsroom](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-us/introducing-the-new-creator-rewards-program/)).
  - Only 9/109 of our TikToks passed the 1,000-view gate.

### #4: Originality guardrails before we apply for YPP
- **Every episode varies the outcome:**
  - Meatball doesn't always "nearly die".
  - No more than 1 in 3 episodes uses peril.
  - Most outcomes are comedic or curious.
- **Every scene has movement.** No more still-image slideshows.
- **Retire the fixed WMS template.** Use it as an occasional special, not a daily series.
- **Strip debug overlays** in render QA.
- **Why:** YouTube's July 2026 guidance names "same situation over and over again with the same outcome", "image slideshows and templated storylines" and "repetitive scenarios of animals in exaggerated distress or peril" as not monetizable ([Tubefilter](https://www.tubefilter.com/2026/07/13/youtube-inauthentic-content-monetization-policy-update/)). Failing review would waste the whole push.

### #5: Own one title pattern and stop using series-tag titles
- **Long-form patterns:**
  - "Meatball's [Organ] When He [everyday thing]"
  - "What Happens If You [do X] to a [house bug]?"
  - Keep the "Why It Sucks To Be Born As…" long-forms only for house creatures (bed bug, roach, dust mite), where our fact packs exist.
- **Shorts:** 4–8 words, a question or a threat. Never the answer.
- Move "(Things Living In Your House #51)" into the description or playlist.
- **Why:**
  - CJnzz Explains built a 118.5K long-form median on one pattern in one sub-niche ([channel](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCG_5iZn64LnZu36rteMA5hA)).

## 8. Proposed new specialist roles

1. **Showrunner / Story Editor.** Owns the series bible (cast, running gags, "his cat, Sauce" rule), the weekly episode arc, the cliffhangers and the varied outcomes (originality guardrail). Signs off on the story before anything is scripted.
2. **Retention Analyst.** Reads per-video data (Metricool YTVP08 AVD, TikTok RPM/retention-at-30/60 s where available), finds where viewers drop, and gives the Scriptwriter and editors 3 cut notes per video within 48 h. Keeps the A/B hook log.
3. **Search Strategist.** Pulls TikTok Creator Search Insights "Content gap" topics and YouTube search terms (vidIQ) each week and turns them into a list of search-answer chapters and episode titles.
4. **Originality & Policy QA.** A pre-publish check against YouTube's inauthentic-content wording (template, repeated peril, slideshow) and TikTok originality, plus a render scan for debug overlays and reposted topics. A gate before every upload, and before the YPP application.
5. **Animator / Motion Lead.** Moves the house style from still scenes to moving scenes: a change every 1–2 s, organ and bug characters. Keeps the cartoon Meatball style the user picked.
