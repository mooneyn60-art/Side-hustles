## 1. Overview and launch sequence

FurEver Faces is dog-themed print-on-demand, published through Printify. The launch set is eight products (Santa Paws ornament, tee, mug, and pet bandana; Doodle Mom tee, mug, and ornament; Doodle Dad tee). Etsy Studio owns the catalog, titles, descriptions, and watch-outs in section 2. Social Studio owns the on-channel video plan in sections 3–6.

The videos have to look like Meatball Myths, not like a gift-shop ad that wandered onto the account: sourced pet facts or an honest "no study in this cut," jokes on Meatball and Noodle, the locked drawn style, no prices, no fake urgency or reviews, and a plain Dec 5 Christmas-delivery cutoff (not a countdown). Shop posts are a **separate cadence** from the channel's educational TikToks.

### Creator Rewards vs shop-tagged posts (verified)

The claim to check was: Creator Rewards excludes sponsored or promotional posts, so the money-making 60s+ educational cuts must stay separate from shop-tagged posts.

**That claim is right in substance, with one precise wording note.** TikTok's official Creator Academy page for the Creator Rewards Program, listed as last updated Sep 10, 2026, says eligible videos must be original, high-quality, and over 1 minute, and that **"Ads, paid promotions, or sponsored content do not qualify."**

- Source: https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/en/article/creator-rewards-program

TikTok Support, in "How is the Creator Rewards Program different from the TikTok Creator Fund?", defines original content as videos that **"do not include Duets, Stitches, or sponsored content."**

- Source: https://support.tiktok.com/en/business-and-creator/creator-rewards-program/how-is-the-creator-rewards-program-different-from-the-tiktok-creator-fund

TikTok Support, in "Promoting a brand, product, or service on TikTok," requires the content-disclosure setting on posts that promote a brand, product, or service, **including "promoting yourself or your own business."** Those posts are labeled **"Promotional content."**

- Source: https://support.tiktok.com/en/business-and-creator/creator-and-business-accounts/promoting-a-brand-product-or-service

I did **not** find a current official TikTok Creator Rewards or TikTok Shop page that literally says "a product tag, by itself, makes a video ineligible." A shop-tagged FurEver Faces video is still a post that promotes our own business, so it should be disclosed as Promotional content. Given that official label plus the official Rewards ban on ads, paid promotions, and sponsored content, do not assume a tagged shop video will earn Rewards.

**Rule for this launch:** keep the 65–90 second educational "Would Meatball Survive?" and "Things Living In Your House" cuts untagged and non-promotional, in the existing 12:00 / 19:00 / 21:30 ET (and already-scheduled 20:15) slots. Run shop-tagged FurEver Faces videos as a separate 15–30 second cadence at 15:00 ET, three to four times a week. Do not add a product tag, a shop CTA, or a promotional disclosure to a Rewards-intended minute cut. After any shop video is posted (and only once Moondog88 has okayed posting), check its Rewards eligibility badge in TikTok Studio and adjust if TikTok's labeling differs.

This 15–30 second shop cadence is an intentional exception to the standing "every TikTok over 61 seconds" rule in `MASTER_ORDERS.md`. That length rule exists so educational posts can qualify for Rewards. Shop-tagged promotional posts are a different class of post and should stay short so they are not mistaken for, or padded into, Rewards cuts.

Do not switch @meatballmyths to a Business Account to open a Shop. Creator Academy and `MASTER_ORDERS.md` both treat Business Accounts as ineligible for Creator Rewards. Confirm that TikTok Shop can be enabled while the account stays Personal before anyone changes the account type.
