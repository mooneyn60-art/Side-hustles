# Fact pack: "Your Life as a Cockroach: 8 Things You Have to Get Through" (cockroach born-as long-form)

Prepared for pitch 2 in `/workspace/drafts/longform-pipeline-pitch-1117.md` and Packaging title claims in `/workspace/packaging/longform-bug-1-4-titles-thumbs-1117.md`. Research date 2026-11-15 (ET). Row IDs CR1 to CR42. Format follows the crocodile pack: ID | fact | exact quote | URL | scope and how it was opened | do not say.

**Lead species: German cockroach, *Blattella germanica*.** Chosen because the born-as spine is the indoor kitchen life, the egg case, and the crowding line: "a single female and her offspring can produce over 30,000 individuals in a year." Those figures are live on UC IPM for German, not American. Do not mix German numbers with American cockroach (*Periplaneta americana*) numbers. The spectacular crack-squeeze (one-tenth of an inch / two stacked pennies) and the headless "weeks" demonstration are American-only and are tagged *P. americana* in CR13–CR16 and CR31–CR32. The pitch's "dime" comparison is CUT.

Labels: [raw] = fetched by curl from the box today and quoted from the extracted text (copies in `/workspace/drafts/facts/.bug-title-src/`). [pdf] = official PDF downloaded today. Date checked for every row: 2026-11-15.

## 0. Read this first
- One lead species. German numbers stay on German. American numbers stay on American. A line that says "a cockroach" or "roaches" may only use a source that is itself species-general, and the script must keep that hedge.
- Egg-case count for the pitch: use UC IPM "about 30 young." Flag UKY "up to 40" and UF "30 to 40" as disagreements; do not blend them into one number.
- Crowding line: use UC IPM "a single female and her offspring can produce over 30,000 individuals in a year," with those conditions. Do not blend with UKY "thousands" / "several thousand offspring in less than a year," and do not hang the 30,000 figure on American (about 12 young; over 800 in a year).
- Crack figures are not interchangeable. German adults "can hide in a crack 1/16 inch wide" (UC IPM). American cockroaches can "squish their bodies to one-tenth of an inch — the height of two stacked pennies," "if highly motivated" (UC Berkeley News, Jayaram / Full). Never say "dime." Never put the 1/10-inch compression on German, or the 1/16-inch hide on American.
- Headless rumor: tell it straight with the SciAm hedges. Do not demonstrate. Tipping used American cockroaches, sealed the wound with dental wax; "A couple lasted for several weeks in a jar."
- Safety: follow the label; keep products out of reach of children and pets; avoid foggers, bombs, and aerosol sprays; do not offer a "safe" amount of a pest or a folk killer; do not treat EPA's "one month without food / a week without water" as a standalone way to wipe out an infestation. Asthma wording stays "can" / "in some individuals," not "will make you sick."
- No spoken citations (Social Studio rule). Each line carries its source in an on-screen tag; the description carries the list.
- Unsafe to imitate: CR31–CR32 and W1 (headless demo). Pesticide misuse and DIY "just starve them / just fog the house" are in Myths and Warnings.

## Hook numbers

Sourced "you + your home + a number or threat" lines. Each is tied to a row. Hedges stay attached; the 12-word hook cap is a script target, not a reason to drop a condition.

1. **About 30 young in a German egg case, in your kitchen.** "Each egg case contains about 30 young" (CR6, UC IPM). Hedges: German; "about"; egg case, not "40 babies." UKY/UF 30–40 stay in the description (D1). Short hook: "A German egg case holds about 30 young." (8 words)

2. **Over 30,000 from one German female and her offspring, in a year.** "A single female and her offspring can produce over 30,000 individuals in a year" (CR22, UC IPM). Hedges: German; "female and her offspring," not "one female"; "over"; "in a year." Do not use UKY "thousands" as the same claim (D3). The official sentence is longer than 12 words; do not shorten it by dropping those conditions.

3. **An adult German can hide in a 1/16-inch crack in your home.** "Adult German cockroaches can hide in a crack 1/16 inch wide" (CR10, UC IPM). Hedges: adult; German; hide, not squish; 1/16 inch, not 1/10 inch. Short hook: "Adult German roaches can hide in a 1/16-inch crack." (11 words)

4. **American, clearly labeled: squish to two stacked pennies, if highly motivated.** "They're about half an inch tall when they run freely, but can squish their bodies to one-tenth of an inch — the height of two stacked pennies" and "they could slip through slits of one-tenth of an inch if highly motivated" (CR13, UC Berkeley News). Hedges: American cockroach (*P. americana*); one-tenth of an inch; two stacked pennies, not a dime; "if highly motivated." Never as "a cockroach" or a German line.

Close fifth, not a hook without its hedges: EPA "without water roaches can die in a week's time" (CR31) is species-general sanitation, not a German killer and not a DIY treatment.
