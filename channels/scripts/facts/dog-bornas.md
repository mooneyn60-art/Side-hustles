Prepared for the dog born-as long-form, the fourth in the "born as" shape after the house cat (research 87), the polar bear (research 98) and the lion (`/workspace/drafts/facts/lion-bornas.md`). Research date 2026-11-15 (ET). Row IDs DB1 to DB37. Format follows the lion pack: ID | fact | exact quote | URL | scope and how it was opened | do not say.

Labels: [raw] = fetched by curl from the box today and quoted from the extracted text (copies in `.dog-src/`). [carried] = row taken from an earlier pack that was checked live on 2026-11-15, re-opened where noted.

## 0. Read this first
- Pet science is hedged: "may", "often", "about", "in one big count". Breed, size and country change the numbers. Never turn one study into "all dogs".
- Health and safety lines keep their condition and their call-for-help wording (DB11, DB17, DB21, DB23). A toxin line says what, says it can be dangerous, and says to call a vet or an animal poison control line.
- This is a pet video. The shelter row (DB13, DB14) and the cancer row (DB29) are said clean, with no joke on the line or right after it.
- No spoken citations (Social Studio rule, 11/15). Each line carries its source in an on-screen tag; the description carries the list.
- Existing material reused: `/workspace/drafts/facts/chocolate.md` (CH-F1, CH-F3, CH-F4, CH-F10: FDA page and ASPCA page re-opened today), `/workspace/drafts/facts/dogs-lick.md` (DL-F1, Penn Vet), and the skills repo's `oh-meatball/research/facts/dog-stare.md` (DOGS1, DOGS5, DOGS6: the Nagasawa 2015 abstract re-opened today). No dog born-as script, outline or pack existed in the skills repo (oh-meatball/, channel-brain/) or in Side-hustles `channels/`.

## 3. Jokes cannot say
- That Noodle (or any dog) ate chocolate, xylitol, grapes or onions and was fine, or got sick.
- A "safe" amount of any food, a safe number of minutes in a car, or a home treatment.
- That a dog felt guilty, sad, loved, jealous or anything else; a reaction line belongs to Meatball.
- That a tactic saved a dog. No joke on the shelter (DB13, DB14), parvo-death (DB11) or cancer (DB29) lines, or right after them.
