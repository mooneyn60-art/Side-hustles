## Traps for QA
- C1 says "100% harmless". The script agrees only that harvestmen aren't venomous (P1). It does NOT say cellar spiders are harmless: no allowed source for that was opened. Do not add it.
- Clemson also says harvestmen "do not have venom glands or fangs, so they cannot (and will not) bite you" (page reader; not scripted). Clemson's sentence on body size ("Males and females tend to have smaller bodies, though females have longer legs") conflicts with other pages I saw in search summaries on which sex has longer legs. Not scripted.
- P6 "harmless" is Penn State's word. A house centipede bite is not covered by the page opened; the script says nothing about bites.
- "Thirty legs, zero rent": "zero rent" is a joke on Meatball's side. It adds no claim. "Eats your pests" is P5 ("including pest insects"), so it must keep the word "including": QA may want "eats pest insects among other things".
- The roast hits the bug and the name committee. No line mocks C1, C2 or C3 writers. "Damn good question" is praise.
- Dropped nothing from the 100 pulled threads except as listed above.
