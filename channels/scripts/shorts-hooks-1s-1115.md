## Timing method

Spoken runtime = word count × 60 / 194.5. A word is a token with letters or digits (contractions, hyphenated words, and numerals count as one). No mp4s were in the sparse clone, so existing runtimes below are this same spoken-word math, plus any `pad` already in the queued v6 JSON. New scripts should use no added silence beyond a tiny loop pad of about 0.08 s, which still leaves each cut under 18.0 s.

Target: 52–58 words → 16.04–17.89 s.

## How each Short is built

- Line 1, from 0.00 s: about 6–8 words, search keyword plus the payoff. No rules intro, no countdown, no spoken study name, year, or sample size.
- Frame 0: the creature already mid-action. The on-screen claim matches the spoken hook in meaning, word for word. Meatball still hops in at about 2.3 s.
- Line 2 (aimed at 3–7 s): the Meatball gag, on Meatball only. It adds no number, danger, feeling, motive, or instruction.
- At least two sourced facts. Hedges stay in the voice (`about`, `typically`, `can`, `may`, `up to`). Sources move to the on-screen tag or the description, not the voice.
- Exactly one open comment question, spoken in the loop line before the final "And." Not yes/no, and not a pick between options. The same question is the corner pill and the pinned comment.
- The last line ends on "And" and runs grammatically into line 1. The last line is staged in the hook room, on the same mid-action creature, so the picture loops into frame 0.
- Safety or action lines are said clean, with the condition still attached, never as the hook and never as the joke.
- No banned words: TRUE, FALSE, Myth, Believe, Cartoons, Lied, "Did you know."
- YouTube: mild words only, at most one per Short, never in the hook.
- Length: 16.0–18.0 s at 194.5 wpm.

## Flags: 1-second payoff, 16–18 s, and MASTER_ORDERS

### 1. First spoken line is the searched question

MASTER_ORDERS wants the searched question in the first spoken sentence. A full question often withholds the payoff and overruns 6–8 words.

**How to satisfy both:** the first spoken line is a compact fusion of the search keyword or short vs-phrase plus the payoff. The full searched question stays in the title, in the first sentence of the description, and on the frame-0 card or a keyword card in seconds 1–4.
