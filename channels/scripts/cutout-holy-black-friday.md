# Cutout episode 2: HOLY BLACK FRIDAY (Nolan 11/19: South Park-style takes on hot topics, don't hold back)
Targets: a fictional scam TV megachurch preacher (greed, fraud, hypocrisy) and a fictional bigoted uncle at Thanksgiving. The joke always lands on the bigot or the hypocrite, never on any race, religion or its believers. Swears spoken. No real people, churches or brands. Cast: Meatball, Pip, Dot, Grandma + new PASTOR CHAD (white teeth, gold suit, headset mic) and UNCLE RICK (sunburn, trucker hat with a flag, folding chair).

[Banner slam: MEATBALL MYTHS / HOLY BLACK FRIDAY] (jingle)
SCENE 1: Thanksgiving table at Pip's house. Uncle Rick at the head, carving a burnt turkey.
1. UNCLE RICK: Before we eat, I just want to say: I'm not racist, but...
2. MEATBALL: Every sentence that starts like that ends with Grandma leaving the table.
   (Grandma stands up, takes the whole pie, leaves)
3. UNCLE RICK: All I'm saying is, people should stay where they came from.
4. DOT: You're from Ohio, Rick. Please go back.
5. UNCLE RICK: I love this country. I've got the flag on my hat, my truck, and my swim trunks.
6. PIP: The tag on your hat says made overseas.
7. UNCLE RICK: (long pause) ...That's a lie spread by the media.
8. MEATBALL: Rick, you hate foreigners, but your whole personality was shipped in a container.
   (TV in the corner flips on by itself: PASTOR CHAD, glowing)
SCENE 2: TV studio, gold stage, a donation phone number spinning.
9. PASTOR CHAD: Friends! The Lord spoke to me this morning. He said, Chad, you need a fourth jet.
10. PASTOR CHAD: And He said the good people watching should pay for it.
11. DOT: God talks to you a lot for a guy with three DUIs.
12. PASTOR CHAD: Sow a ninety-nine dollar seed, and the Lord will bless you with this seventy-five-inch TV!
13. PIP: That's the same TV from MegaMart.
14. PASTOR CHAD: The Lord shops sales, Pip.
15. UNCLE RICK: (already dialing) Finally, a man of God who gets me.
16. MEATBALL: Rick. He just took your rent money live on TV and called it a blessing.
17. UNCLE RICK: That's between me and my lord and savior, Chad.
SCENE 3: The church parking lot. A long line of people holding checks. Grandma cuts in front of all of them.
18. GRANDMA: I've gone to church every Sunday for sixty years. I go for the free coffee and to judge the hats.
19. GRANDMA: (to Pastor Chad) Your hat is shit, sweetie.
   (police sirens; Pastor Chad in handcuffs, still smiling, gold suit shining)
20. PASTOR CHAD: This isn't fraud! It's a miracle with paperwork!
21. DOT: It's a pyramid scheme with a choir.
22. UNCLE RICK: So I'm not getting the TV?
23. MEATBALL: No, Rick. But you did get scammed by a guy in a gold suit, so you finally have something in common with everybody.
SCENE 4: back at the table, dawn, everyone eating cold pie with their hands.
24. MEATBALL: You know what this taught us? ...Nope. Not a damn thing. Pass the pie.
25. PIP: Same time next year?
26. ALL: Obviously.
[End card: WHO'S THE WORST PERSON AT YOUR THANKSGIVING TABLE? 👇]
