It prefers kitchens and bathrooms, favoring warm (70° to 75°F), humid areas close to food, water, and dark areas for harborage. Severe infestations may spread to other parts of buildings. Of the cockroach species in California, it is the most persistent and troublesome, and may pose health concerns from food contamination and indoor allergens (UC IPM). They are commonly found in homes, apartments, condominiums, and commercial food establishments (Texas A&M).

This pest thrives in the presence of humans but does not occur outdoors (University of Kentucky). Adults are fully winged but rarely fly (Texas A&M). This video does not say they cannot fly.
