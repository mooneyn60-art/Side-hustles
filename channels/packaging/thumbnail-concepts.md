# Thumbnail concepts (draft)

Status: draft only. No art export, repo edit, post, or Studio A/B upload from this file.

Sources: Social Studio audit (the five on-image lines named in the brief); MASTER_ORDERS / KNOWLEDGE white-background thumbnail recipe; "The bridge" and Shorts cover-frame rule; Studio A/B is long-form only, up to 3 variants, winner by watch-time share. Nolan adds challengers after post.

## Recipe this file follows

- White or plain background.
- One huge drawn subject (the creature, the pet, or the object of the curse). Check readability as `NAME_168.jpg` (readable at 168 px wide).
- 3–4 words max on the image (Studio brief; the written recipe already caps at 2–4). Hand-lettered, not a system font.
- Exactly one word in red (the curse or the twist). The other words stay dark so they hold up small.
- One hand-drawn arrow, pointing at the thing that matters.
- No Meatball in the thumbnail. He shrinks to a brown dot at 168 px (MASTER_ORDERS / research on the Meatball-in-thumbnail skip). Several audit sketches put Meatball in frame; those are rejected here and flagged below.
- No custom Shorts cover frames (MASTER_ORDERS + audit). These concepts are the 1280×720 (or 16:9) YouTube thumbnail, or a long-form A/B card. They are not a Shorts cover.
- `_b` / `_c` challengers are for long-forms only. YouTube Test & Compare does not run on Shorts. Each concept below still records a `_b` / `_c` idea in case a long "who would win" or a holiday long-form is built later (research 116 allows a long Bears vs Lions video "if the Short shows demand"). If the piece stays a Short, shoot only `_a` and do not set a cover frame.
