**Title rules checked on every option:**
- The creature is named in the first 40 characters, or the title carries the series tag.
- A hedge is kept wherever the source hedges.
- Each title has one odd fact and one emoji.
- Each title is 100 characters or fewer, counted in UTF-16 units. The count is shown as `[n]`.
- No banned words. No "sucks". Statement form.

**Where the sources live:** every claim cites a repo fact file or script under `/workspace/skills-readonly/skills/oh-meatball/` (shortened below to `dh/` = `scripts/drawn-house/`). The pitch also requires each fact pack to **reopen these pages live** before a script is locked. A title is final only after that live check.

**Born-as titles:** the pitch's locked post format is "Your Life as a X: 8 Things You Have to Get Through" (no emoji, no tag). The options below keep the "Your Life as a" lead and add one sourced odd fact plus an emoji, as this brief asks.
