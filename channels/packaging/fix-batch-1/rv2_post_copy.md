## 2. rv2_daddy-longlegs_retention-v2.mp4 (17.59 s)
Title: Daddy Longlegs: The Most Venomous Spider? True or False
Description:
You've heard daddy longlegs are the most venomous spiders. False. Two different animals get that name: the harvestman isn't a spider and has no venom, and the cellar spider has venom but there's no proof it hurts people. The "fangs too short to bite" part is a myth too.

Full video: Don't Kill the Spider in Your Corner. It Eats Bigger Spiders https://youtu.be/KWOM2-mNmww
