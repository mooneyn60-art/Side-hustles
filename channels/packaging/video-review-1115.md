# Video review, 11/15 (5 videos, watched with Studio stats)

## Why the winners win
- "3 Bugs You Should Never Kill" (CTR 5.44%, 42.7% viewed) and Daddy Longlegs Short (96.6% viewed) deliver the twist within 4-6 seconds.
- Weak long-forms stall: rules / "keep score" / countdowns before content (Wolves content starts 0:22; Weird Facts has 3-second silent countdowns every fact).
- Custom 2D animation (3 Bugs, House Cat) holds; stock photos + white text slides (Wolves, Weird Facts) feel like a slideshow.
- Study names, years and percentages read aloud kill momentum (House Cat 0:19 "2007 study of 979 cats").

## Fixes per video
- Wolves: new cold open "This wolf will eat you. This 'wolf' eats your socks. How did a wild predator end up on your couch?" Cut the checkpoint rules (0:16-0:21), the Europe-vs-Siberia tangent (0:50-1:15), and trim the cave-art run (4:46-5:21). Animate stills (zooms/cutouts).
- Weird Animal Facts: delete every 3-2-1 countdown, cut the 7-second rules (0:13-0:20), stamp TRUE/FAKE on screen instead of white slides, cut to 4-5 minutes. Trim hummingbird torpor (8:00-8:15).
- House Cat: move the 5-week socialization beat to #1; drop the 2007 genetics study from the open. Keep studies in the description Sources and on-screen source tags, but say them plainly ("scientists found...") instead of reading years and sample sizes aloud. Same for the 1997 weight study at 4:25.
- 3 Bugs (keep): cut the 1849 Pennsylvania tangent (0:18-0:22), animate the asthma beat at 2:35, add comment prompt at the Kill Pile (~1:30): "Which of these bugs makes you panic most?"
- Rule for all long-forms: main promise by 0:05, no rules or "keep score" intros, no silent countdowns, a shot change every 2-4 seconds, one comment prompt mid-video.

Note: keep every fact sourced and cited; only how it's spoken changes.
