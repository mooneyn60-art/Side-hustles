## Patterns
1. **The creature is the star, not a face.** 8 of 10 have no face at all. The creature fills ~35–60% of the frame in 7 of 10. The only faces (1, 3) are small cartoon people.
2. **Short warning or number text.** 8 of 10 have a headline, 1–5 words (most often 3). The top performers by views-per-sub use a command or warning (WAIT!, LEAVE HER ALONE, DON'T MAKE THIS MISTAKE, RUN) or a number (9 MINUTES).
3. **Cartoons break out on tiny channels.** The two biggest outliers vs subs are illustrated: Zyka (10.6K subs, 2.45M views) and Benny Goes Wild (7.41K subs, 630K views).
4. **Extreme backgrounds.** Either near-white (2, 3, 9, 10) or near-black (4, 6, 7, 8).
5. **Grids and no-text macros read worst at 168 px** (3, 8, 10).
6. **Direct EP5 comp:** Forgotten Lin's bathtub giant-house-spider video got 10,387 views vs 997,633 for the same channel's porch video, so "spider in the bath" alone isn't the hook.
