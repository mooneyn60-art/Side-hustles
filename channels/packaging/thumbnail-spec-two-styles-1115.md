# Thumbnail spec: two styles — "TOO SLOW" (chase) and "PLAY DEAD?" (question) — 11/15

Draft only. No Studio upload, no repo edit. Source: Moondog88's plan update 11/15 (`/workspace/drafts/plan-update-1115.md`, decision 4) and Jarvis's samples `thumb_B_illustrated.png` / `thumb_B2_illustrated-question.png` (`/workspace/meatball-animation-v2/samples/`). 1280×720, check at 168 px wide.

## Shared rules (both styles)
- **Words:** 2–3, hand-lettered heavy caps, thick black outline. **Exactly one red word** (the threat or the twist). No banned words: TRUE/FALSE, Myth, Believe, Cartoons, Lied, "Did you know" (plus no "sucks" without Nolan's yes). Any number or claim on the image must already be in the title's source; the hedge lives in the title, so the image never states a claim stronger than the title.
- **168 px test:** export `NAME_168.jpg`. Every word and the threat's face must read; if not, cut a word.
- **Safe zones:** keep words, faces and the arrow out of the **bottom-right 20% × 15%** (timestamp badge) and the bottom 4% (progress bar). Words go top-left or top-right, never bottom-right.
- **Frame-0 match:** the thumbnail art = the video's frame 0 (threat in close-up, same pose, same angle, same colors), so the click promise pays off in the first second. Export the thumbnail from the frame-0 panel, then add words. Spoken line by 0:05 repeats the thumbnail words.
- **One huge subject:** the threat fills ≥ 50% of the frame height.

## Style 1 — "TOO SLOW" (chase art)
- **Use when:** something is coming at you or you can't get away (chase, attack, escape, speed, distance).
- **Layout:** full-bleed illustrated scene (no white field). Threat charging toward camera, upper-center/right, mouth or claws open, motion dust/speed lines. Victim (Meatball, or "you" POV) big in the lower-left foreground, scared face. Words top-right on the open area.
- **Colors:** saturated scene; words yellow (#FFD21F) with black outline, the red word #E0201B with yellow or black outline. No white field.
- **Arrow:** normally **none**; speed lines do the pointing. Use one hand-drawn red arrow only when the threat is small (a bug), pointing at it from the words.
- **Words pattern:** verdict on the chase — e.g. `TOO SLOW`, `8 FEET AWAY`.

## Style 2 — "PLAY DEAD?" (question art)
- **Use when:** the video answers "should you…? / what do you do?" (squish it, run, play dead, wash it, leave it).
- **Layout:** words stacked big on the left third; art on the right two-thirds on a plain light/white panel: the threat looming over the subject in the moment of the decision. Question mark always on the last word.
- **Colors:** white or pale panel (keeps the old white-background recipe here); first word dark green or black, the **red word is the action in question** (`DEAD?`, `SQUISH?`).
- **Arrow:** optional, one red hand-drawn arrow from the question to the small threat (bugs, spiders). Not needed when the threat is big (bear).

## What changed from the 11/15 recipe (plan wins)
| Old recipe | New (Moondog88's plan) |
|---|---|
| White background always | White/pale only for PLAY DEAD?; TOO SLOW is a full-bleed scene |
| 2–4 words | **2–3 words** |
| Arrow always | Arrow only when the threat is small; chase uses speed lines |
| No Meatball | Meatball allowed **only if large** (≥ 35% frame height, as in both samples); never as a small figure (brown dot at 168 px). House-bug thumbnails keep the bug as the subject and leave Meatball out |
| Subject drawn | Illustrated panel art (plan decision 3), same panel as frame 0 |

## Which style fits which video
| Video type | Style | Example words (red word in caps-bold) |
|---|---|---|
| Chase / danger, Would Meatball Survive? | TOO SLOW | `TOO **SLOW**`, `DON'T **RUN**` |
| "Should you / what do you do?" survival | PLAY DEAD? | `PLAY **DEAD?**` |
| House bugs — invasion, distance, speed, "it's coming for you" | TOO SLOW (bug close-up + red arrow) | `8 **FEET** AWAY` |
| House bugs — kill or keep, wash, treat | PLAY DEAD? | `**SQUISH** IT?`, `WASH **HOT**?` |
| Spiders — identify / leave alone | PLAY DEAD? | `**SQUISH** IT?` |
| Spiders — hunting/jumping action | TOO SLOW | `**40X** JUMP` |
| Myth corrections (paused topics aside) | PLAY DEAD? as a question, never "TRUE/FALSE" or "MYTH" | `**PLAY DEAD?**` |
| Born-as (house bug or animal) | TOO SLOW if the episode's frame 0 is the threat chasing "you"; PLAY DEAD? if it's a choice | per episode |
