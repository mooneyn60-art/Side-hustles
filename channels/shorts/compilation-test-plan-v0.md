## Policy to follow (cite, do not paraphrase as a loophole)

YouTube channel monetization policies, including the July 15, 2025 rename of "repetitious content" to **"inauthentic content"**:

https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392

That page, read Nov 15, 2026, says:

- Monetized content should be the creator's original creation. If content is borrowed, it must be changed significantly to make it the creator's own.
- Content should not be mass-produced, generic, repetitive, or manipulative.
- **Inauthentic / generic or repetitive content:** channels that feel interchangeable from video to video, or that appear produced from a template, are not allowed to monetize. Image slideshows, templated storylines, or scrolling text with minimal or no narrative, commentary, or educational value are called out. Same intro/outro is allowed when the bulk of each video is different; a series may follow a pattern if each video has a distinct storyline, focus, or concept.
- **Reused content is a separate policy** on the same page: repurposing content already on YouTube or another source without significant original commentary, substantive modifications, or educational or entertainment value. "Clips of moments … edited together with little or no narrative" and "Short videos you compiled from other social media websites" are examples of what is not allowed to monetize. The reused-content rule applies even with permission, and it is separate from copyright. What is allowed includes edited footage from other creators where a storyline and commentary are added, and content that demonstrates substantive editing unique to the channel.

This compilation must be built to clear **both** bars: new narration, a real ranking argument, and visible editorial work — not a concatenation of already-posted Shorts.

## Hard rules for this test

1. **No raw re-uploads and no raw stitches.** Do not drop existing Shorts end-to-end under a new title. Do not compile with only a new intro bumper.
2. **New narration** throughout, in the locked Andrew voice, written from already-checked fact packs. No new unsourced numbers.
3. **Ranking context** is the reason the video exists: an ordered "every bug in your house" argument (for example least to most, or "never kill" vs "must act"), with a spoken reason for each placement. The packaging plan already names *3 Bugs in Your House You Should Never Kill (And 4 You Must)* as the one long-form format that currently clears CTR and hold. This test should sit in that family, not as a clip dump.
4. **Each segment must be rewritten for the ranking**, not reused as the Short's original hook-plus-loop. New picture or clearly re-cut picture is preferred over a full-screen replay of a posted Short.
5. Length target from the packaging plan: **10–15 minutes**, and only if retention can hold. That is a plan figure, not a proven compilation length for this channel.
6. House bugs and spiders only if this ships inside the Nov 16–29 topic test; otherwise wait until Scriptwriter and Social Studio place it on the calendar. Compilations still need Moondog88's okay before a post.
7. Do not invent a ranked list in this skeleton. The order, the count, and the spoken reasons are Scriptwriter's, from sourced packs only.
