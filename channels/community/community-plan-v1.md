## 2. Comment-reply playbook

Apply on YouTube (and, when a person is reading TikTok comments by hand, the same rules). Do not post any of these until Moondog88 wants replies to go out. Live comment text was not available to tools on Nov 15, so this is a playbook, not a reply to anyone specific.

### Voice

Curious, warm, and self-deprecating — as Meatball, not as a lecturer. Facts stay hedged. Jokes land on Meatball (his sheets, his flashlight, his cereal), never on the viewer, a group, or a pet's feelings. YouTube replies stay clean: no swears in this playbook (pins and titles already ban them; keep replies in the same clean band). Attributions and safety lines stay clean and keep their condition. Do not invent an ID, a count, or a treatment. Do not promise "Meatball explains the best comment next" unless the next batch will actually do it (MASTER_ORDERS).

### What to heart

- A real story that answers the pinned question (a closet, a tree, a window sill, a cupboard).
- A polite correction that names a source.
- An on-topic "I think I have this bug" comment that is not asking for medical or extermination advice.
- Kind jokes aimed at Meatball.

Heart those. A heart is not a medical OK, an ID, or a treatment plan.

### What never to engage with

- Spam, scams, follow-for-follow, or bot piles.
- Harassment, slurs, or insults aimed at viewers or groups.
- Requests for medical advice, diagnosis, or pest-control plans beyond what a sourced video already says. Do not add a vacuum, spray, or humidity "cure" that the checked line does not support. The hooks draft already flags dust-mite and booklice humidity lines as not a treatment guide.
- Venom, bite, or sting emergencies — see the one conditional reply below, then stop. Do not debate symptoms.
- Politics, betting, or sports-picks (including Bears vs Lions as teams; MASTER_ORDERS: no logos, players, or betting).
- Other creators' drama.
- The shop: Etsy, Shopify, TikTok Shop, merch, discount codes, or "where can I buy this." Do not promote or argue. If needed, one clean close: "This channel is just the videos." Then stop.
- Demands to remove the AI label, or to ID a bug from a photo with certainty in the comments.

### If someone says they were bitten or thinks it is an emergency

Reply once, keep it conditional, and do not diagnose:

> I'm not a doctor, and this isn't medical advice. I can't tell from a comment what bit you. If you or someone else was bitten and you're worried, contact a doctor or your local poison control center.

Do not name a species as the cause. Do not say whether a bite is "harmless" or "serious" in a reply. If they keep asking, do not keep the thread going.

### Corrections

Thank them, do not argue, and take it back to the source:

> Thanks for flagging that. I'll check it against the source in the description and come back if the wording needs a hedge or a fix.

If the source already supports the video, say so calmly and quote the hedge ("typically," "about," "can," "may," "in one study"). If the source does not, get the line fixed through the normal second-model path — do not win the comment thread.

### Turning replies into future Shorts

Live comment text is not in Metricool or in the public page fetches used on Nov 15. To mine comments, export from YouTube Studio (or grant a comments-read path), then log candidates by hand.

**Promotion criteria (all of these should be true):**

- The same question, or a close search-shaped wording, shows up more than once — not a one-off personal ID.
- It fits a current show: *Things Living In Your House* (pets or house creatures) or *Would Meatball Survive?*
- It can be answered with a primary source and a hedge, not with a guess, a feeling, or a treatment plan.
- It is not a medical, legal, or emergency question.
- It would make a 16–18 s Short or a mid-video comment prompt, with one open question (not yes/no).
- A fact pack can be built before any script is written (MASTER_ORDERS: research first).

**Do not promote:** bite/venom cases, "is this going to kill me," brand or shop requests, other creators' clips, or news that breaks the channel's news rules.
