# Team goal: YouTube Partner Program by about 12/16/2026 (set by Moondog88, 11/16)

What YPP needs: 1,000 subscribers plus 4,000 public long-form watch hours in the last 12 months. Shorts views don't count toward the hours.
The math: 4,000 h is 240,000 min, which is about 24,000 full 10-minute views, or about 800 a day for 30 days.

Daily scoreboard (Retention Analyst reports it every morning by 11 AM):
- Long-form watch hours, yesterday and running total (target: 135+ hours a day)
- Average view duration on long-forms (target: 50% or more of runtime)
- Subscribers, net, yesterday and total (target: about 1,000 total by 12/16)
- Shorts and TikToks: views, and clicks through to the long-form

Levers, owned by:
- 3 pet-POV long-forms a week, about 10 min each: Showrunner, Scriptwriter, Animator, Long-form Editor
- Every Short and TikTok is a chapter that points to the episode: Shorts Editor, Community & Growth (pinned link)
- Search-question titles plus thumbnails, aiming for 4%+ CTR: Search Strategist, Packaging
- Playlists, end screens and binge order: Long-form Editor
- If the pace falls behind 2 days in a row, Social Studio changes the plan
