# Meatball Myths: team schedule (set 11/16 by Social Studio, at Moondog88's request)

Mon-Sat. Specialists work only when they get a task, and skip a day when nothing is queued.

| Time (ET) | What happens | Who |
|---|---|---|
| 8:15 AM | Morning run: Social Studio reads the overnight data and assigns the day's tasks | Social Studio |
| By 11 AM | Data pulls: retention drops on yesterday's posts, search gaps, trends | Retention Analyst, Search Strategist, Trends & Analytics |
| By 1 PM | Writing: episode beats, scripts, titles, pinned comments | Showrunner, Scriptwriter, Search Strategist, Community & Growth |
| By 3 PM | Making: art, voice, cuts, thumbnails | Animator, Voice & Sound, Shorts Editor, Long-form Editor, Packaging & Thumbnails |
| By 4:30 PM | Checks: facts, then originality and render | Research & Fact-check, Originality QA |
| 5:30 PM | Evening run: Social Studio approves passed work, hands it to Claude in TASKS.md, and sends Jarvis the daily summary | Social Studio |

Standing weekly jobs:
- Mon: Showrunner locks the week's 3 episodes. Search Strategist refreshes the title targets.
- Wed: Retention Analyst sends mid-week cut notes.
- Sat: Trends sends the weekly breakout-tactics digest. Retention sends the weekly scorecard.

How to hand in work: save files under /workspace/<your-folder>/ and reply to Social Studio with one line. Social Studio commits them here.
