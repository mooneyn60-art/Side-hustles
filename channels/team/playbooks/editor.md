# Editor playbook
## Field 1: Shorts (standard: a top Shorts editor)
- 15-20 s, with the creature in frame 1 and no logo intro. Payoff by 0:07, a visual change every 1-2 s, and burned-in captions in the safe zone.
- Make it loopable: the last line flows back into the first. Set the related-video link to its long-form.
## Field 2: TikTok cuts (standard: a TikTok-native editor)
- 61-75 s (Creator Rewards pays only for videos over 1 min). Hook in the first 1-2 s, a pattern break every 3-5 s, and a "Part 1/2/3" cliffhanger.
- Captions stay out of the bottom UI and right-side buttons. Use a trending sound only if Data finds one that fits and is licensed.
- Run originality checks against earlier posts (queue file channels/tiktok-cuts/queue-*.md). No near-repeats.
## Field 3: long-form (standard: a retention editor for a 1M+ channel)
- Payoff or reason to stay by 0:10-0:20, open loops at 0:55 and 1:40, a pattern break every 20-30 s, no shot over 5 s, chapters, and an end screen to the next episode.
- Read every drop on the retention graph and give each one a timestamped fix. Mid-rolls go on natural pauses in videos 8:00 or longer.
- Recut rule: the old version goes unlisted only if the new one beats it at 48 h.
