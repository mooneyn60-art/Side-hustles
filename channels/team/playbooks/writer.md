# Writer playbook
## Field 1: showrunning (standard: a cartoon head writer, like Bluey or Kurzgesagt-style series planning)
- Series beat formats: House Guests (weekly, same cold open, catchphrase and slot), Roast Your Roommate (viewer request), "Would Meatball Survive?" (stakes), and a monthly 15-20 min tentpole.
- Slate math: every long-form gets 2-3 feeder Shorts and 2-3 TikTok cuts planned at the outline stage.
- Pick topics from Data's outliers (proven demand) and give each its own angle. Never copy a title.
## Field 2: scripts (standard: a sitcom room)
- Hook of 11 words or fewer that opens a question, with the creature on screen in frame 1. Payoff by 0:07 (Shorts) or 0:20 (long-form). Open loops at 0:55 and 1:40.
- A joke every 5-7 s with the facts as the punchlines. Use rule of three, misdirection, callbacks and character reactions (Noodle panics, Sauce is deadpan, Meatball is confidently wrong).
- Character voice: each line should only work for the character saying it.
- Long-form at about 155 wpm. Read it aloud and cut any line that's only information.
- Shorts end on a cliffhanger that points to the long-form. TikTok cuts end on "follow for part 2".
## Field 3: titles, descriptions, community (standard: a top YouTube packager)
- Titles are a search question or a curiosity gap, under 60 characters, matching the thumbnail without repeating it. Write 3 per video, ranked, using Data's keywords.
- Description: the first 2 lines are the hook plus the keyword, then chapters and sources.
- Pinned comment asks a question that's easy to answer. First-hour reply drafts are in Meatball's voice, and Studio approves them before anything is posted.
- Mine comments for the top 3 Short ideas each week.
