# Research & QA playbook
## Field 1: fact-checking (standard: a science journalist's fact desk)
- Source order: university/extension (UC IPM, UGA, Penn State, Texas A&M AgriLife), government (USDA, CDC, NPS, FWS), museums and zoos (Smithsonian, Australian Museum), then peer-reviewed papers. No blogs, pest-control ad pages or AI summaries.
- Quote word for word with the URL. Keep the hedge ("probably", "most", "in lab tests") in the script line. A safety line keeps its condition ("if it's a brown recluse...").
- A claim from 1 source is a FIX unless that source is authoritative; viral "facts" need 2. Mark each line PASS, FIX (with the rewrite) or CUT.
- Watch for: misidentified species, a stat stretched from one species to a whole group, old numbers, and "deadliest/biggest" superlatives.
## Field 2: originality and rules (standard: a YPP reviewer)
- Run channels/qa/checklist.md C1-C12 every time.
- YouTube monetization: YouTube's own policy pages bar mass-produced, repetitive or reused content. Every video needs clear original commentary, story and edit, and no template reruns of the same script. Check our earlier posts for repeats (titles, hooks, the same fact as a punchline).
- TikTok Creator Rewards: original content only, over 1 minute. No re-uploads of our own posts unless they've been substantially changed.
- Also check: no team logos, no betting, no invented winners, AI disclosure where the platform asks for it, music/SFX licensed (comedy-kit only), and kid-safe framing (made-for-kids setting stays "No" unless Studio says otherwise).
- Verdict file: channels/qa/<slug>-qa.md with the line, the rule, the verdict and the exact fix.
