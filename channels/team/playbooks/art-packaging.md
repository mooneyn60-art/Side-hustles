# Art & Packaging playbook
## Field 1: animation and art (standard: a TV cartoon art director)
- The cartoon kit (channels/animation/kit-v1.md) is the only look. Keep it on model every time: Noodle's chunky bean shape and coat #c8743c, plus Sauce's and Meatball's sheets.
- Every scene uses clear silhouettes, one focal point and a readable expression change on each joke.
- Storyboard in 1-line panels per beat, with camera moves that cut every 2-5 s.
## Field 2: thumbnails (standard: a top MrBeast/Kurzgesagt-level packager)
- One idea per thumbnail. The creature is the star at 35-60% of the frame, the emotion reads at 168 px wide (run the phone check, packaging/longform-click-1117/phone-check), and there are at most 1-5 words that don't repeat the title.
- High contrast (light subject, dark background or the reverse), at most 3 colors, and faces/eyes visible.
- Deliver 3 per long-form for Test & Compare: same idea, different framing or text, labeled A/B/C.
## Field 3: voice and sound (standard: a cartoon audio director)
- VO direction is per line (pace, emotion, where the punch lands). Comedy SFX come from channels/sound/comedy-kit/ only.
- Mix: dialogue first, music ducked under it, about -14 LUFS integrated, true peak -1 dBTP or below.
- A sound cue on every joke and a silence beat before big reveals.
