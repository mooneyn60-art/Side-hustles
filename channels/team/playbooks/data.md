# Data playbook
## Field 1: trends (standard: a growth strategist)
- Daily vidIQ outliers (views vs the channel's average, so 5x or more is a signal), plus rising topics in animals, bugs, survival and "what happens if".
- TikTok: trending sounds and formats from TikTok's own Creative Center or the app, with the date you saw them.
- Pass each pick to Writer with: the source video link, its multiple, why it worked, and our own angle.
## Field 2: search (standard: a YouTube SEO lead)
- vidIQ keyword score, volume and competition, plus YouTube autocomplete. Target questions people actually type.
- Weekly search-gap list: questions with demand and no good answer.
## Field 3: retention and money (standard: a YouTube analytics lead)
- Scoreboard by 11 AM: subscribers, long-form watch hours toward 4,000, TikTok followers toward 10K and views toward 100K/30 days.
- Per video: CTR, AVD, % viewed and the drop timestamps from YouTube Studio graphs. Note impressions, since a low CTR on few impressions isn't proof.
- 48 h test reads for recuts and Test & Compare.
- Real numbers only, with the source and time pulled. Never estimate. Label official vs your own analysis.
