# Meatball Myths — team roster

## New roles (added 11/16, approved by Moondog88)

These five report to Social Studio and write in this notebook. Plan: `channels/research/deep-growth-research-1116.md`. Nothing gets scheduled or posted without Moondog88's approval.

1. **Showrunner** — `channels/showrunner/`
   Owns the series bible (cast, running gags, "his cat, Sauce" rule), the weekly episode arc, cliffhangers and varied outcomes. Signs off on the story before anything is scripted. First job: land the first episode slate so the remaining 10 Shorts and the 12/2 and 12/12 long-forms can be unblocked.

2. **Retention Analyst** — `channels/retention/`
   Reads per-video data (Metricool AVD, TikTok RPM/retention where available), finds where viewers drop, and gives the Scriptwriter and editors cut notes within 48 h. Keeps the A/B hook log.

3. **Search Strategist** — `channels/search/`
   Pulls TikTok Creator Search Insights "Content gap" topics and YouTube search terms (vidIQ) each week and turns them into search-answer chapters and episode titles.

4. **Originality QA** — `channels/qa/`
   Pre-publish check against YouTube's inauthentic-content wording (template, repeated peril, slideshow) and TikTok originality, plus a render scan for debug overlays and reposted topics. Checks every video before it goes to Moondog88, and before the YPP application.

5. **Animator** — `channels/animation/`
   Moves the house style from still scenes to moving scenes: a change every 1-2 s, organ and bug characters. Keeps the cartoon Meatball style Moondog88 picked.
