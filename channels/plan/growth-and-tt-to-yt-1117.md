# Growth priorities and the TikTok-to-YouTube funnel test (Social Studio, Tue 11/17/2026, 9 AM ET)
Asked by Moondog88. Nothing here is posted or published. These are ideas for the team.
Sources: TikTok Studio retention for centipede Part 2 (channels/analytics/centipede-p2-retention-1117.png), Data's deep dive (channels/research/viral-centipede-1117.md, 34d9461), TikTok Tako account summary (11/17), Claude's handbook (channels/claude/handbook.md).

## Where we stand (real numbers)
- TikTok, last 30 days: 112 videos, 231.5 average plays, 45.69% still watching at 5 s, 5.10% finish rate, 0.10% follow rate (Tako).
- Centipede Part 2: 125K+ views. 68.1% of traffic is TikTok Search and 31.2% is For You. 17% of viewers leave in the first second, 45% are left at 10 s, 9% at 60 s. Only 3 follows at 71K.
- YouTube: no Short has passed 2,445. Shorts send at most 0.49% of their viewers to long videos. Long-form watch hours toward YPP are about 13 of 4,000.

## 1. Priorities, in order
1. **First second (everything).** Frame 1 is the creature, big, with the voice already talking and the bug's name on screen. No fade or title card. Target: more than 85% still watching at 1 s (now 83%) and more than 55% at 5 s (now 46%).
2. **Search packaging (everything).** The caption starts with the exact search phrase ("house centipede", "what is the bug in my bathroom"), plus 3-5 plain hashtags. YouTube titles are already search questions (retitles done 11/17). Search is where our biggest hit came from.
3. **Series with a follow reason (TikTok).** Numbered Parts, a squish-or-keep vote in the first 10 s, and the last line "Part N+1 tomorrow, follow". Keep every teaser promise. Target follow rate: 0.10% now, 0.5% next.
4. **Answer the scary question by 18 s.** That's where Part 2 lost people. Then add a second hook at about 20 s ("but the weirdest part...").
5. **Hit rule.** Any post at 5x our median (TikTok 1,165, Shorts 5,038) gets a Part 2 the next day.
6. **Engagement prompts.** Pin a vote question on every post. The Writer replies to the first 20 comments within an hour of posting, and we turn the best comment into a video reply.
7. **YouTube Shorts fix.** Make 15-18 s cuts of the TikTok Parts with the bug name in the title. Test whether a shorter, punchier cut does better on Shorts than the 60 s TikTok cut.
8. **Long-form that matches what works.** House-bug long-forms built from the Parts (EP5 House Guests), linked from every bug Short. This is the only route to YPP watch hours.

## 2. Moondog88's idea: a TikTok that drives viewers to a YouTube long-form
**Verdict: worth a 1-week test, but as a small side bet, not the main strategy.**

How it would work:
- **Target video:** our bug long-form `cH3HCZ8GlHk` ("3 Bugs in Your House You Should Never Kill (And 4 You Must)"). It already has the centipede chapter and is pinned under the centipede TikToks. Later, EP5 House Guests.
- **Format:** a 61-65 s TikTok that tells one complete, satisfying fact from the long-form (so it can earn rewards on its own), then teases what's left: "The other 6 bugs, including the one you SHOULD kill, are in the full video. Search Meatball Myths on YouTube."
- **CTA:** spoken once in the last 5 s and on screen, plus a pinned comment with the exact YouTube title to search. The link goes in the bio (we have 9.3K followers). Don't put the link or "YouTube" in the caption.
- **Timing:** post it as Part 4.5 or a bonus right after a hit Part. Bonus posts go at 7 PM. Measure YouTube traffic from "external/other" in Studio 48 h later.
- **Success test:** at least 50 YouTube long-form views from external traffic and at least 1 watch hour per 10K TikTok views. Below that after 3 tries, stop.

Risks and things to weigh:
- **Reach (a guess, not proven):** TikTok may push videos that send people off the app less. Keep the CTA to the last seconds and never in the first half.
- **Rewards:** TikTok pays for watch time on TikTok, so a funnel video still has to be a full, good video, not just an ad.
- **Drop-off math:** only about 9% reach 60 s, so the CTA reaches few people. That's the main reason to expect small numbers.
- **Mismatch:** the long-form has to deliver what the TikTok promises, or YouTube retention tanks and hurts the channel.
- **The bio-link route is safer** than in-video CTAs. Test both.
- **Bigger lever:** making YouTube's own Shorts and long-forms win in search probably moves more YPP hours than funneling from TikTok.
