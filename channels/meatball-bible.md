How he talks: he reads the checked fact or credit first, clean, then the joke or reaction after it in the same line (MASTER_ORDERS, "Make them funnier" and the 11/11 swearing order). ## Recurring bits, and Noodle and Sauce

Meatball's dog is **Noodle**. His cat is **Sauce** (Nolan's pick, 11/12; the names are his to change). From 11/12, every pet video plants one of them early and pays it off late. Always an action on screen, never a feeling or motive said as fact:

- **Sauce** does the opposite of what Meatball wants and walks off.
- **Noodle** ends up with Meatball's food or prop (the turkey, the camera, the towel, the snacks).

Say the name with "his cat" or "his dog," comma form, the first time in each video. Meatball stays the lead; the pets do not get a last-line lecture.

## Hook formula

For every new Short and TikTok cut from 11/12 (MASTER_ORDERS, "Make them funnier" and the later search-led order):

1. The first spoken line is the searched question (the phrase people type). A search card can sit on screen for the first 2 to 2.5 seconds on TikTok.
2. Line 2, starting in the 3–7 second window, is an acted Meatball gag with a prop or a card. It is not the species name or the anatomy.
3. The last line flows into the hook, in the hook's room, usually ending on "And." The pinned comment is the same question as the corner pill. No spoken or visual outro (no Subscribe, Follow, or end card).

YouTube Shorts run 16–20 seconds with at least two sourced facts plus a payoff. Sweary TikTok minute cuts run 65–92 seconds.

## What jokes may never do

A joke never adds a number, a danger or health claim, an animal's feeling or motive, or a safety instruction the checked line does not support (MASTER_ORDERS; the user's edit of cats-turkey minute line 8 — "Put the skin down, Meatball. Fucking hell." in place of "Fucking raccoon energy." — is the same rule: do not imply a pet motive). Jokes land on Meatball, never on the viewer or a group, and never inside a sourced credit. No slurs. No "you" in an insult. Facts do not change to make the joke work.
