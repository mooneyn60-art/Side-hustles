### Working
1. **Breaking-news explainers on TikTok, posted within hours of the event.** At 40–51 h: "why the ground stop happened" 17,600 plays; first airline-outage video 9,340; Jensen Huang 3,457; Microsoft outage 3,034. The airline Shorts had 271, 99 and 100. *Source: OM/research/117.* Follow-ups that add no new event fail (Jensen part 2: 107; "still on" update: 360). *Source: OM/research/117; OM/research/102.*
2. **The funny pass on YouTube Shorts is a lead, not yet a proven win.** At about 10 h, the funny Shorts on 11/12 ran about 1.7× the plain Short of the same day at the same age: moose 893 at 12.7 h against the plain false widow's 519 at 13.4 h. *Source: OM/research/102.* In Windsor, the winners held 0.43–0.54 engaged share and the Shorts that got swiped sat at 0.30–0.39. *Source: OM/research/115.*
3. **Topic is the one big lever found in Shorts.** House bugs and spiders had a median of 1,146 views against 474 for other animals (p 0.001). The four older survival Shorts had 1,124. In about 166 comparisons, hour, weekday, gap, number per day and title tricks showed no effect. *Source: OM/research/100-growth-deep-dive.md (summarised in CB/MASTER_ORDERS.md "Research loop").*
   - Note: Nolan moved new videos to pets and WMS on 11/11 ("speak to the majority"). The first read of pet/WMS Shorts against bug Shorts of the same age is **Wed 11/18**. *Source: CB/MASTER_ORDERS.md.*
4. **A Short gets its views on day 0.** A median 85–90% of lifetime views arrive on the first day, and about 1% after day 2. *Source: CB/KNOWLEDGE.md (research 90, 100).* So timely hooks only pay if the Short posts on the day.
5. **Search is the second traffic source on YouTube Shorts.** It brings 5.5% of Shorts views (about 140 a day) at a higher engaged share: 50.0% in search against 43.8% in the feed. *Source: CB/KNOWLEDGE.md (research 94).* People type questions like "can dogs eat turkey", "why do cats purr", "why does my dog stare at me". *Source: OM/research/95-next-topics.md.*
6. **The best long-form so far** is "3 Bugs in Your House You Should Never Kill": 257 min, 208 views, 43.6% average viewed in 28 days. Next is "Weird Animal Facts" at 141 min but only 11.0% viewed. *Source: OM/data/longform-hours.csv.*
