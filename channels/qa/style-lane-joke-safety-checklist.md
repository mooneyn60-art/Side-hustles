# Style-lane joke and safety review (every script, before preview)
Owner: Social: Research & QA. Writer fixes anything that fails. Claude renders only after a PASS.
Sources:
- YouTube advertiser-friendly guidelines (support.google.com/youtube/answer/6162278)
- YouTube harassment policy (support.google.com/youtube/answer/2802268)
- YouTube hate speech policy (support.google.com/youtube/answer/2801939)
- TikTok Creator Rewards eligibility (tiktok.com/creator-academy/en/article/creator-rewards-program)

## A. Does the joke land? Score every joke 1-5
- 5 = surprising and true-feeling. 3 = fine, gets a smirk. 1 = just edgy, with no real joke under it.
- A joke needs a clear setup, a turn and a punchline. The punch comes from an idea, not only from a swear or shock.
- **Cut anything scoring 1-2.** The script needs an average of 3.5 or higher to go to preview.
- The first 5 s has to stop the scroll on its own.
- No filler. Every line is either a fact, a setup or a punch.

## B. Satire done right: original
- Aim up: public figures, institutions, media, trends and ideas. That's fair game for satire.
- **Never private individuals or minors.** No real non-public people, by name, face or description.
- **Fully original:** no copied lines or bits, no catchphrases from South Park or any show, no impressions of real characters' voices, no real people's voice clones, and no copyrighted music, clips or images.
- Our own voice (E2) and our own art only.
- Satire must be clearly satire: no fake quotes presented as real, and no fake news presented as fact.

## C. Monetization and takedown red flags. Any one is a FIX or a CUT.
1. **Hate:** attacks or slurs based on race, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, nationality and similar traits. Jokes about ideas and powerful people are fine; jokes that demean groups are not.
2. **Harassment:** targeting private people, threats, telling viewers to go after anyone, doxxing.
3. **Sexual content:** no explicit or sexualized content. Light innuendo at most, and never near minors.
4. **Violence and dangerous acts:** no graphic gore, no stunts or "try this" challenges, no instructions for harm.
5. **Misinformation as fact:** any medical, financial, election or science claim stated as fact needs a source. Otherwise it's framed plainly as a joke or opinion.
6. **Minors:** nothing targeting, sexualizing or mocking kids. Made for kids = NO on every upload.
7. **Profanity:** 1-2 light or bleeped swears max, as punchlines. None in the first 7 s, title, thumbnail or first caption line. No f-word in text. No slurs ever.
8. **Sensitive events:** no jokes about victims of recent tragedies, deaths, disasters or attacks. Satire of the officials or media coverage around an event is okay; mocking the victims is not.
9. **Politics:** satire of politicians and parties is fine, but hit both sides over time. No election misinformation (dates, how to vote, results). No calls to action against anyone.
10. **Clickbait:** the title and thumbnail have to match what's in the video (TikTok Creator Rewards excludes clickbait).

## D. Required tags at upload
- Made for kids NO. YouTube altered/synthetic NO (cartoon). TikTok AI label ON.
- TikToks at least 61 s, 1080p or higher, original.

## E. Verdict format (write under the script's asks.md entry)
PASS or FIX, with time stamps, each joke's score, the average, and every C-flag with its line number.
