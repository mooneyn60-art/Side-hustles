# Originality and monetization QA checklist: Meatball Myths

Owner: QA (final originality check). Applies to every script and every render before it goes to Moondog88. Drafted Mon 11/16/2026 (ET). Policy pages were opened live on 11/16/2026 unless a row says otherwise.

**How to use it:** run every row. A row is PASS, FIX (it blocks until fixed), WATCH (no block, but log it at channel level) or N/A. If any row is FIX, the whole verdict is FIX. Each verdict file quotes the offending line and gives replacement wording.

## A. YouTube (YPP and Shorts)

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Y1 | Not templated or interchangeable | Put the script next to the last 5 in its series or lane. Compare the hook fact, the gag, the verdict and the visuals. | Each video has its own fact, storyline and payoff. A shared intro, outro or format is fine when the bulk of the video differs. | Swap in a new fact or angle, rewrite the gag, or vary the payoff. Never ship a near-duplicate. | [1311392, Generic or Repetitive](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392) |
| Y2 | Same character, same situation, same outcome | Across the series, log the outcome of every episode ("Meatball lived", "probably not" and so on). | A character is not put "in the same situation over and over again with the same outcome". Avoid a stock ending on every episode. | [1311392, Generic or Repetitive](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392) |
| Y3 | No repetitive animals in peril or distress (team rule). Dated July 2026, press-reported. | Check every shot list and visual note for an animal hurt, trapped, in distress, or "rescued". Count those scenarios across the last 10 uploads. | No animal is shown in exaggerated distress or peril. No staged rescues. Threat close-ups of a calm or moving animal are fine. Danger is told through sourced facts, not shock. | Re-board the shot to show the animal calm or neutral and carry the danger in cards. ## B. TikTok (Creator Rewards and the For You feed)

| ID | Check | How to verify | Pass criteria | Fix if failing | Citation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| T1 | 1-minute rule | Read the rendered TikTok cut's duration. | TikTok's minimum is at least 1 minute. The house rule is ≥61 s: clean cuts run 61–75 s and sweary cuts 65–92 s. A 16–20 s YouTube Short never posts to TikTok. | Build a separate minute cut, or keep the video YouTube-only. | [US Terms §2](https://www.tiktok.com/legal/page/global/creator-rewards-program-us/en) ("at least 1 minute"); [Creator Academy](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/en/article/creator-rewards-program) ("over 1 minute"); [MO L201–202](https://github.com/mooneyn60-art/skills/blob/082e9752fa806dbd358f07c7f8f73f3eda55600e/skills/channel-brain/MASTER_ORDERS.md#L201) |
| T2 | The minute is made of new content, not padding | Check how the cut reaches 61 s. | It reaches 61 s with new lines and new shots. No looping the Short, no slowed or sped audio, no freeze frames, no slideshow. | Write more sourced beats and gags. Never loop. | [EEA Terms](https://www.tiktok.com/legal/page/global/tiktok-creator-rewards-program-eea/en) ("original content … produced entirely by the creator", "high quality"); Creator Academy (ineligible: "low quality … slide videos") |
| T3 | Original, not reused | Check for other creators' clips, watermarks, Duet or Stitch, or Photo Mode. | Everything is our own drawn animation and narration.
