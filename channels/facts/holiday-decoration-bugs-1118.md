# Fact pack: bugs in holiday decorations and stored boxes (slug holiday-decoration-bugs-1118)

Research date: 2026-11-18. Status: PARTLY SOURCED. 20 facts. Real trees and natural decor are well sourced (Extension). "Bugs in your stored decoration boxes" is NOT sourced as a holiday topic: no Extension page opened is about holiday boxes. Facts F16 to F20 are general stored-item and attic pests (carpet beetles, silverfish). Linking them to decoration boxes is our inference, labeled GUESS below.

## 0. Read this first
- MAIN FACT: most real trees have no bugs, and the ones that do are rare and mostly harmless (F1, F2, F3). The Christmas tree flagship already runs on this ground.
- THE ONE REAL WATCH-OUT: egg cases and egg masses (praying mantis, spotted lanternfly). Check before the tree comes in (F3, F4, F7, F9).
- Hedge it: "most", "rare", "usually".
- GUESS (not sourced): "your decoration boxes are full of bugs". The sources say carpet beetles and silverfish live in lint, paper and damp storage. Only say what F16 to F20 say, about attics, cardboard and out-of-season items, not about decorations.
- Not found: how many families find bugs on trees (only Illinois's one-in-100,000 line, F2, with no study named on the quote).

## 2. Three hook facts
1. F1 + F2 + F3: nearly every tree is clean, but check for a mantis egg case first.
2. F7 + F9: a spotted lanternfly egg mass can hatch in your living room in a few weeks if you bring it in.
3. F10 + F11: the bugs in a tree are sleeping for winter and wake up in your warm house (Purdue says "may").
