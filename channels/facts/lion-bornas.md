Prepared for: Meatball Myths long-form, third in the "born as" shape after the house cat (research 87) and the polar bear (research 98). Research date: 2026-11-15. Row IDs LB1 to LB43. Same table format as research 98 (ID, fact, exact quote, URL, scope, do not say).

How each source was opened:
- [raw] = fetched today with curl; the sentence copied from the page text.
- [abstract] = the paper's abstract, pulled today from Europe PMC (the paper itself not read).
- [fact sheet] = San Diego Zoo Wildlife Alliance (SDZWA) Library fact sheet pages. These are bullet notes that cite papers; the quote is the bullet as printed, with its citation. The cited paper was NOT opened unless a row says so. Treat as a zoo summary of the literature.
- [secondary] = a news report quoting an agency, used only because the agency page was blocked. (No row uses it now: LB41 moved to SANParks on 2026-11-15.)
- Date checked for every row: 2026-11-15.

## Lessons carried from research 88 and 98 (writer and checker)
1. The checker opens the page, not this pack.
2. Every percentage says what it is a share of and where.
3. A survival or mortality figure names its source and place; the Smithsonian and SDZWA figures are different measures (D1).
4. A safety line keeps its trigger and is said clean, credited, with the joke after.
5. No single number where sources differ: roar distance (D3), lifespan (D2), tenure (D4), lion count (D5), hunt success (by place only, LB19).
