## 0. Read this first
- MAIN FACT (F1, F2, F3): wood warms up indoors, the bugs in it wake up, and they come out. University of Maryland Extension: store only a day or two of firewood inside.
- BIG CALM-DOWN (F12, F16, F18): Extension says these bugs are nuisances. Termites in firewood do not start a nest in the house.
- Hedge it: "most" and "generally" are in the sources. Do not say "never".
- Not found: how many bugs a typical log holds (no count on any page opened). A claim that firewood bugs "infest your house" is NOT supported; the pages say the opposite.

## 2. Three hook facts
1. F1 + F3: the wood warms up, the bugs think it is spring, so keep only a day or two inside.
2. F8 + F16: termites in firewood will not start a house nest (two Extensions agree).
3. F20: firewood moves invasive bugs hundreds of miles.

## 3. Traps
- Do not say "bugs infest your home from firewood". F12, F18 say little to no threat.
- Termite wording: Illinois Extension also says termites "may tunnel into and feed on firewood that has been stacked on the ground" (same Illinois page, quote from page reader). That is about the pile outside, not the house. Keep the two apart.
- No product, spray or dose advice (F14 says do not spray).
- Dropped: the USDA APHIS firewood page (404) and the Oregon State winter-insects page (403). Neither was opened.
