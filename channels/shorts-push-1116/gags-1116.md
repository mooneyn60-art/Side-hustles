# Shorts push 11/16: physical gags + shot changes (stage directions only)

Asked by Social Studio on 11/16 because the videos feel boring and Meatball spends too long in thinking and pointing poses. These are stage directions only. Every spoken line, fact, card, safety line and verdict is the same as in the lane files, word for word. Run times stay as they were (16.35 to 17.89 s), and check.py still reports ALL HARD CHECKS PASS. Times come from the spoken-line timing check.py uses (194.5 wpm).

Rules for every Short:
- One physical gag per Short, acted under the existing spoken gag line. It shows actions only. No stated or captioned feeling, no face cards, no words added.
- Meatball's gags happen at home only. He never shares a shot with the real animal, and no spider or bug is touched. Fake webs are party streamers, tape or Halloween decoration.
- Cuts fall about every 2 to 3 s, landing on line breaks. A few line-break cuts are 1.6 to 1.9 s, and no shot runs past 3.1 s.
- A safety card stays pinned on screen across its cut, so the line is never off screen while it is spoken.
- Frame 0 and the final loop shot use the same framing, so the loop stays seamless.
