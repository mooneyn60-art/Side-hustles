## 4. Verdict (provisional, n = 6 against n = 40)
1. **Views: level, not better.** New pets and WMS have a median of about 922 (n=6, ages 16–51 h). Older bugs and spiders had 1,100 (n=40, all at 48 h or more). The channel's newest-12 median is 932.
   - That is inside the noise. The ages are not matched, and the bug baseline was measured before the 11/11 dip. So no call on "better or worse" yet.
2. **Spread: the pets look steadier.** The six readable pet and WMS Shorts all landed between 873 and 1,100. Bug and spider Shorts in the baseline ranged from 48 to 2,300, and 11/11 had several under 100 (`OM/research/115`). That is a lead, not proof, at n=6.
3. **WMS: no call (n=2 new).** The older survival Shorts matched bugs on views (median 1,100, n=4) but held viewers less (engaged share 0.377 against 0.467).
4. **What to make:**
   - **More:** pet Shorts built on searched questions (the cat sweet, cat purr and dog turkey types). Nothing argues against them and they don't flop.
   - **Hold steady:** WMS until at least 5 drawn ones have a 48 h read.
   - **Less:** bugs. This was already ordered on 11/11 (`skills/channel-brain/MASTER_ORDERS.md`, "Direction change"), and the data gives no reason to reverse it.
   - **Stop:** cover frames, until the stuck dog guilty-look Short is explained in Studio.
